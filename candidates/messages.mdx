Every application has one message thread with the company. Open the **Messages** tab on your application to read it, or start it yourself if nobody's written to you yet.

## Reading and replying

Messages show newest at the bottom. Type your reply in the box underneath and click **Send**, or press Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac). You can format your reply with bold, italic, bullet lists and links.

## One thread, however you reply

Replying by email works the same as replying in Staffer. Reply to the email you got, and your answer lands in the same conversation. Use whichever is easier. The recruiter sees it in one place either way.

## No attachments

You can't attach a file to a message, in Staffer or by email. If a step in the process asks you for a document, that's a separate upload, not a message. See [Screening steps](/candidates/screening-steps).

## A note on AI

A recruiter might use Staffer's AI to help write a reply. Either way, the message is sent by the company, in their name.

## Notifications

Most messages from a recruiter arrive by email. A recruiter can also send a message only inside Staffer, so check the **Messages** tab on your application too. The tab shows how many messages are in the thread.
