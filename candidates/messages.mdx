Each application has its own message thread with that company's recruiter.

## Replying

Reply from the application view. It goes straight back to the recruiter on that same thread, no separate email client needed.

<Info>
  A message drafted by the company's AI copilot carries a short disclosure line so you know when that's the case.
</Info>
