Settings has one section: Data & Privacy. Everything about what a company can see and what happens to your data lives here.

## Exporting your data

Download everything Staffer holds about you in one export.

## Revoking one company

Remove a specific workspace's access to your profile without affecting anything else, if you're done with one company but still active elsewhere.

## Deleting your account

Request full account erasure and everything is scheduled for deletion after a cancellation window, not removed instantly. Change your mind before that window closes and the request is cancelled, nothing lost.

<Warning>
  Once the cancellation window passes, erasure is irreversible.
</Warning>
