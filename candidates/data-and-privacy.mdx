Everything about your data lives in one place: **Privacy & data**, under Settings. Sign in and you'll see every company that has anything on file for you. Open a link from a company's email instead and you'll see only that one company, with a banner telling you so.

## Companies with access to your data

Staffer lists every company that has ever touched your profile, whether you applied to them, they added you as a candidate, or they hold a contact address for you. For each one you'll see how many applications you have there, when it was last active, and any email address on file along with where it came from: found through a paid lookup the company ran, added by the company directly, or shared by you.

Next to each company:

- **Also future roles** widens your consent, so this company can consider you for roles beyond the one you applied to.
- **Withdraw consent** pulls your consent for this company entirely. It clears your consent on every open application you have with them at once, meant to stop them from continuing to process you. This needs your profile to be fully confirmed. See [Claim your profile](/candidates/claiming-your-profile).

## Remove a company's access

Below the consent controls, **Request revoke** opens a short form (a reason helps but isn't required) with a **Send revoke request** button. You'll see a **Revoke pending** badge until the company acts on it.

Once the company approves it, your open applications there close and the contact details they added for you are deleted. They can still see your past applications, including notes and messages. To have those deleted too, ask the company to erase your data.

You can only request this for a company that already has something of yours: an application, or a contact record. And if your profile is only partly confirmed, the request is saved but never actually reaches the company. Get fully confirmed first if you want it acted on.

## Download your data

**Get a copy of your data** is signed-in only. Click **Download my data** and you get a ZIP immediately, no waiting, no link to click later. It contains a short readme and one spreadsheet covering your profile, your claim history, every application, your answers to any screening steps, how you were scored, and which companies hold contact details for you.

## Delete your account

At the bottom, **Request full erasure** starts the process:

1. Your profile disappears from every company's search and every public listing right away.
2. Nothing is actually deleted yet. You have 30 days to change your mind with **Keep my data**.
3. After 30 days, deletion runs automatically and can't be undone: your profile, your applications, anything you've uploaded, and your ability to sign back in are all permanently removed.

This needs a fully confirmed profile, and you have to be signed in to request it. A private application link isn't enough. If you're only partly confirmed and file a request anyway, it's recorded but nothing happens until you're fully confirmed.

<Warning>
  Once the 30 days pass, erasure is final. There's no recovering your profile or applications afterward.
</Warning>

A company you dealt with keeps a bare record: which stage each application reached and when, and how it was scored, so they can show their hiring process happened. Everything that identifies you is removed first: your name, contact details, messages, notes about you, and interview recordings and transcripts.

## How long a company keeps your data

Each company sets its own rules for how long it keeps old or inactive applications. If a company's rules flag your application, you may get an email asking whether you'd like to renew your consent, with a one-click link, before anything is deleted.

One rule is fixed and the same everywhere: recordings and transcripts from an interview are deleted six months after the application closes, no matter which company it was or what their own retention rules say. Nothing else about that step, like which stage it was and when it happened, is affected.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What needs my profile to be fully confirmed?">
    Withdrawing consent, erasure and revoke requests. You can file an erasure or revoke request while partly confirmed, but it won't go ahead until you're fully confirmed. Downloading your data only needs you to be signed in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If I ask a company to stop, does that delete my Staffer account too?">
    No. Removing one company's access only affects that company. Your profile and any other applications you have elsewhere are untouched. Delete your account separately if you want everything gone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my mind after requesting erasure?">
    Yes, up until the 30 days pass. Click **Keep my data** on the same page any time before then.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
