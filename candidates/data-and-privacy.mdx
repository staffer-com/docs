Everything about your data lives in one place: **Privacy & data**, under Settings. Sign in and you'll see every company that has anything on file for you. Open a link from a company's email instead and you'll see only that one company, with a banner telling you so.

Every email you get through Staffer links to the privacy policies that apply and to a **Manage your data** page, so you always know where to go. If the link has expired, the page offers you a way back in.

## Who can find your profile

If you created your profile yourself on Staffer, by applying with a CV or with **Set up manually**, employers can't find it in search unless you choose to let them. In **Privacy & data**, under **Your profile on Staffer**, turn on **Let employers on Staffer find my profile**. Employers using Staffer can then find your profile in search and contact you about roles. Turn it off any time, and you'll see "Your profile is hidden from search".

- It's off unless you turn it on. Profiles like this that already existed were hidden from search too.
- Applying to a job never turns it on. The company you apply to still gets your application.
- The switch only shows when you're signed in.
- A profile Staffer built from public sources has no switch. To take one of those out of search, erase your data (below).

## Companies with your data

Staffer lists every company that has ever touched your profile, whether you applied to them, they added you as a candidate, or they hold a contact address for you. For each one you'll see when it was last active and what it holds about you: data from your applications there (or only your profile, if you never applied), any contact details and where they came from (a third party, you, or the company itself), and your consent to be considered for future roles, with the date it ends.

Every action for a company sits in its **…** menu:

- **Consider me for future roles** lets this company keep you in mind for other jobs, not only the ones you applied to. The menu shows what you agree to: the company "may keep your details for 12 months to consider you for future roles and contact you about them. You can withdraw this at any time." You can renew it when it ends.
- **Stop considering me for future roles** takes that back, for every application you have with this company at once. The company can then only use your data for the applications you already have. This needs your profile to be fully confirmed. See [Claim your profile](/candidates/claiming-your-profile).
- **Stop emails from**, followed by the company's name, stops that company emailing you. See below.

You're offered the same choice when you apply. At the end of a process, unless you were hired, your application's **Process** tab offers it again in a card like "Stay on Acme's radar", with the company's name and a **Consider me for future roles** button.

## Stop emails from one company

You can stop one company emailing you without withdrawing anything. Pick whichever is easiest:

- On your application, open the **Privacy** tab. Under **Emails**, click **Stop emails from**, followed by the company's name, then **Stop emails** to confirm.
- Here in **Privacy & data**, open the company's **…** menu and choose **Stop emails from**, followed by its name.
- Click the unsubscribe button your email app shows on one of their emails. That stops them in one click. If the link opens in your browser instead, Staffer asks you to confirm with **Stop emails**.

In **Privacy & data**, the company's row then shows **Unsubscribed**. The company sees that you've unsubscribed, and can't email you through Staffer about any of your applications there. That includes rejection emails, interview booking confirmations, application confirmations and reference reminders. Messages they write still appear on your application, and your applications carry on as before. This doesn't need a fully confirmed profile.

To get their emails again, click **Allow emails again** on the **Privacy** tab, or **Allow emails from**, followed by the company's name, in the **…** menu.

## Remove your data from a company

**Remove my data from**, followed by the company's name, in the company's **…** menu, asks you to confirm. You can add a reason, but you don't have to. Click **Request removal**, and the company shows **Removal requested** until it's done.

The company stops emailing you straight away, before anyone reviews the request. While the request is open, the menu shows **Emails stopped** and you can't turn emails back on.

Staffer reviews the rest of the request first. Once it's approved, the company deletes the contact details it holds for you, and any application still open there is withdrawn. The company can still see your past applications, including notes and messages. Your Staffer profile stays. To remove it everywhere, erase your data (below).

You can only request this for a company that already has something of yours: an application, or a contact record. If your profile is only partly confirmed, the request is saved but nothing happens until you're fully confirmed.

## Download your data

**Get a copy of your data** is signed-in only. Click **Download my data** and you get a ZIP immediately, no waiting, no link to click later. It contains a short readme and one spreadsheet covering your profile, your claim history, every application, your answers to any screening steps, the data requests you've made, and which companies hold contact details for you.

A sheet called "Companies with your data" lists every company that holds your data, with three columns:

- **Company**
- **How they got your data**: for example "You applied", "They found you in Staffer search" or "They added you"
- **How long they keep it**: that company's deletion rules, or "No automatic deletion rule set. Ask them, or ask us to remove your data."

The download doesn't include scores or assessments. Each company holds its own, so ask them for a copy. Use **Request a copy of your data** on the **Privacy** tab of your application there.

## Delete your account

At the bottom, under **Danger zone**, **Erase my data** starts the process. To confirm, type your email address (if Staffer has one for you) and the phrase `erase my data`. A reason is optional. Then:

1. Your profile disappears from every company's search and every public listing right away.
2. Nothing is actually deleted yet. You have 30 days to change your mind with **Keep my data**.
3. After 30 days, deletion runs automatically and can't be undone: your profile, your applications, the contact details companies hold for you, anything you've uploaded, and your ability to sign back in are all permanently removed. The page shows the **Deletion date** until then.

This needs a fully confirmed profile, and you have to be signed in to request it. A private application link isn't enough. If you're only partly confirmed and file a request anyway, it's recorded but nothing happens until Staffer has confirmed the profile is yours. The page tells you how.

<Warning>
  Once the 30 days pass, erasure is final. There's no recovering your profile or applications afterward.
</Warning>

A company you dealt with keeps a bare record: which stage each application reached and when, and how it was scored, so they can show their hiring process happened. Everything that identifies you is removed first: your name, contact details, messages, notes about you, and interview recordings and transcripts.

## How long a company keeps your data

Each company sets its own rules for how long it keeps old or inactive applications. If a company's rules flag your application, you may get an email asking whether you'd like to renew your consent to be considered for future roles, with a one-click link, before anything is deleted.

Interview recordings, transcripts and assessments go with the application. They're deleted together with it, under that company's retention rules, so there's no fixed date. To see each company's rules, download your data (above).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What needs my profile to be fully confirmed?">
    Stopping a company from considering you for future roles, erasure and removal requests. You can file an erasure or removal request while partly confirmed, but it won't go ahead until you're fully confirmed. Downloading your data only needs you to be signed in. Stopping a company's emails needs neither.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If I ask a company to stop, does that delete my Staffer account too?">
    No. Removing one company's access only affects that company. Your profile and any other applications you have elsewhere are untouched. Delete your account separately if you want everything gone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my mind after requesting erasure?">
    Yes, up until the 30 days pass. Click **Keep my data** on the same page any time before then.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
