Everything about your data lives in one place: **Privacy & data**, under Settings. Sign in and you'll see every company that has anything on file for you. Open a link from a company's email instead and you'll see only that one company, with a banner telling you so.

Every email you get through Staffer links to the privacy policies that apply and to a **Manage your data** page, so you always know where to go. If the link has expired, the page offers you a way back in.

## Companies with your data

Staffer lists every company that has ever touched your profile, whether you applied to them, they added you as a candidate, or they hold a contact address for you. For each one you'll see when it was last active and what it holds about you: data from your applications there (or only your profile, if you never applied), any contact details and where they came from (a third party, you, or the company itself), and your consent to be considered for future roles, with the date it ends.

Every action for a company sits in its **…** menu:

- **Consider me for future roles** lets this company keep you in mind for other jobs, not only the ones you applied to. It lasts a year, and you can renew it.
- **Stop considering me for future roles** takes that back, for every application you have with this company at once. The company can then only use your data for the applications you already have. This needs your profile to be fully confirmed. See [Claim your profile](/candidates/claiming-your-profile).

## Remove your data from a company

**Remove my data from**, followed by the company's name, in the same menu, asks you to confirm. You can add a reason, but you don't have to. Click **Request removal**, and the company shows **Removal requested** until it's done.

Staffer reviews the request first. Once it's approved, the company deletes the contact details it holds for you, and any application still open there is withdrawn. The company can still see your past applications, including notes and messages. Your Staffer profile stays. To remove it everywhere, erase your data (below).

You can only request this for a company that already has something of yours: an application, or a contact record. If your profile is only partly confirmed, the request is saved but nothing happens until you're fully confirmed.

## Download your data

**Get a copy of your data** is signed-in only. Click **Download my data** and you get a ZIP immediately, no waiting, no link to click later. It contains a short readme and one spreadsheet covering your profile, your claim history, every application, your answers to any screening steps, how you were scored, and which companies hold contact details for you.

## Delete your account

At the bottom, under **Danger zone**, **Erase my data** starts the process. To confirm, type your email address (if Staffer has one for you) and the phrase `erase my data`. A reason is optional. Then:

1. Your profile disappears from every company's search and every public listing right away.
2. Nothing is actually deleted yet. You have 30 days to change your mind with **Keep my data**.
3. After 30 days, deletion runs automatically and can't be undone: your profile, your applications, the contact details companies hold for you, anything you've uploaded, and your ability to sign back in are all permanently removed. The page shows the **Deletion date** until then.

This needs a fully confirmed profile, and you have to be signed in to request it. A private application link isn't enough. If you're only partly confirmed and file a request anyway, it's recorded but nothing happens until Staffer has confirmed the profile is yours. The page tells you how.

<Warning>
  Once the 30 days pass, erasure is final. There's no recovering your profile or applications afterward.
</Warning>

A company you dealt with keeps a bare record: which stage each application reached and when, and how it was scored, so they can show their hiring process happened. Everything that identifies you is removed first: your name, contact details, messages, notes about you, and interview recordings and transcripts.

## How long a company keeps your data

Each company sets its own rules for how long it keeps old or inactive applications. If a company's rules flag your application, you may get an email asking whether you'd like to renew your consent to be considered for future roles, with a one-click link, before anything is deleted.

One rule is fixed and the same everywhere: recordings and transcripts from an interview are deleted six months after the application closes, no matter which company it was or what their own retention rules say. Nothing else about that step, like which stage it was and when it happened, is affected.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What needs my profile to be fully confirmed?">
    Stopping a company from considering you for future roles, erasure and removal requests. You can file an erasure or removal request while partly confirmed, but it won't go ahead until you're fully confirmed. Downloading your data only needs you to be signed in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If I ask a company to stop, does that delete my Staffer account too?">
    No. Removing one company's access only affects that company. Your profile and any other applications you have elsewhere are untouched. Delete your account separately if you want everything gone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my mind after requesting erasure?">
    Yes, up until the 30 days pass. Click **Keep my data** on the same page any time before then.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
