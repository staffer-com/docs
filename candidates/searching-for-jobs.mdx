**Job Match** looks beyond your own applications, at open jobs across the market rather than only the ones posted on Staffer. Find it from **Find matches** on your dashboard.

## Search by asking, not typing into a box

There's no search box that runs a new search directly. Instead, ask your copilot for what you want, for instance "senior frontend roles in Oslo" or "product jobs at fintechs or AI startups." It searches the market from several angles at once and merges everything into one list, saved as a card titled with a short summary of what you asked for. You can also ask broader questions, like how many UX jobs are open right now, and get a real count instead of a guess.

The search box on the Job Match page itself only filters results you already have, by title, company, or city. It doesn't start a new search, and neither does the **Type** or **Country** filter next to it.

<Note>
  Job Match currently covers Norway. Your copilot will say so if you ask about jobs elsewhere.
</Note>

## Your shortlist

Bookmark a job to add it to your shortlist, a group that always sits at the top of the page. Unbookmark it, or use **Mark as applied** from a job's menu, to take it off again. Marking it applied is a way to clean up your own list, since Staffer has no way to know whether you actually applied somewhere else.

## Nothing here is saved to your account

Both your search results and your shortlist live only in your browser tab. Close the tab and they're gone. Reload the page and they're still there. Your conversation with the copilot works the same way. See [Your copilot](/candidates/copilot).

## Applying to a Job Match result

Clicking a job, or its **Open listing** option, opens the company's own job page in a new tab. That's outside Staffer, so nothing is tracked on your account. If the role happens to be posted on Staffer itself, applying there works normally and shows up in [your applications](/candidates/tracking-your-application). See [Applying to a job](/candidates/applying-to-a-job).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I closed my browser and my matches are gone. Is that a bug?">
    No. Job Match results and your shortlist are only kept in that browser tab, on purpose. Ask your copilot again when you're back.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Job Match cover jobs outside Norway?">
    Not yet. Ask your copilot and it'll tell you plainly if a search you want falls outside what it currently covers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I clicked a job and it left Staffer. Did that count as applying?">
    No. Job Match results link out to the job elsewhere on the web. You still need to apply there yourself. Only jobs posted on Staffer create an application you can track here.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
