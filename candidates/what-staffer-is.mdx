Staffer is the hiring platform companies use to run their recruiting, from writing the job to interviewing candidates. If a company reached out to you, or you applied to one of their jobs, this is where you'll see everything about it.

## Why did a company contact me?

Staffer holds public professional profiles, things like your work history, skills, and location, for people in Europe and North America. Companies search these profiles when they're hiring, similar to searching a professional network. A recruiter likely found yours that way and reached out because they thought you could be a fit for a role.

Staffer's own database doesn't hold email addresses or phone numbers as part of those profiles. If a company has your contact details, they either looked yours up individually, at a cost to them, or you gave your details to them yourself, for example by applying to a job or replying to a message. [Your data and privacy](/candidates/data-and-privacy) lists exactly which companies have something on file for you and where it came from.

## It's free

Using Staffer doesn't cost you anything. There's no fee to have a profile, track an application, or use your copilot.

## What you can do here

Once you're signed in, you can:

- Follow your applications and see what's next: [Track your applications](/candidates/tracking-your-application)
- Complete steps a company asks for, like questionnaires or interviews: [Screening steps](/candidates/screening-steps)
- Reply to a company, or start a conversation: [Messages](/candidates/messages)
- Keep your profile accurate, since companies see your current version: [Your profile](/candidates/profile-and-resume)
- Control who has your data, and delete it if you want: [Your data and privacy](/candidates/data-and-privacy)
