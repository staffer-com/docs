Whether a recruiter found you, you applied directly, or a friend sent you a link, you land in the same place: one profile, one dashboard, one view of every application you have open.

## One profile, wherever you came from

Your profile draws from public information plus anything you've added or corrected yourself. Your own edits always take priority and never get overwritten by anything found later.

## Real status on every application

See exactly what stage each application is at, what's been asked of you, and what's next, instead of waiting on an email that might not come.

## An AI copilot on your side

Ask it about a company, search for jobs, check where you stand on an application, or get feedback on your profile against similar candidates. Anything that changes something real, like withdrawing an application, always asks you to confirm first.
