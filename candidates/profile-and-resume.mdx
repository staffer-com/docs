Your profile is what companies see when they search Staffer or look you up on an application. Open **Edit resume** from your dashboard to see and change it.

## What you can edit

- **Headline**: a one-line summary, shown right under your name.
- **Open to work**, **Engagement**, **Work mode**, **Relocation**: short preferences, each a simple choice like Yes, No, or Unspecified.
- **Location**: your city and country.
- **Languages**: add as many as you like, and mark one as primary.
- **Career summary**: a few sentences on what you do and what you're looking for.
- **Skills**: add or remove them freely, one at a time or several at once.

Changes sit in a draft until you click **Save**. **Discard** throws them away and puts back what was last saved. If you reload the page or navigate away by accident, your unsaved edits are still there when you come back to the same browser tab.

## What's locked, and why

Your name, email, phone number, and LinkedIn URL don't save if your profile is only partly confirmed. Staffer needs to be sure you're really the person behind the profile before letting you change the details a company would use to reach you. See [Claim your profile](/candidates/claiming-your-profile) for what fully confirmed means and how to get there. If you're already fully confirmed and one of these still won't save, email support@staffer.com.

There's no field for your full name on this page, even when you're fully confirmed. If you're fully confirmed, ask your copilot to change it.

## Experience and education

Staffer shows your work history and education as a read-only timeline, pulled from your public profile. You can't add or edit individual roles or schools here yet.

## No photo, no resume upload

There's no way to add a photo or upload a resume or CV to your profile. Your photo, if you have one, comes from the same public sources as the rest of your profile. If you want to send a company your CV, attach it when you apply to one of their listings, not here. See [Applying to a job](/candidates/applying-to-a-job).

## What a company sees

What you edit shows up straight away, to companies searching Staffer and on your profile in any job you're part of. Fields you haven't edited show the public information Staffer has, which can change as public sources update. It never overwrites something you've set yourself.

There's one exception. When a company adds you to a job, or you apply to one, Staffer keeps a copy of your profile as it was at that moment. Editing your profile afterward changes what companies see from then on, but not the copy saved with that application.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I edit my name?">
    There's no field for it on this page. If your profile is fully confirmed, ask your copilot to change it. It checks with you before saving.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why won't my email or phone number save?">
    Your profile is probably only partly confirmed. See [Claim your profile](/candidates/claiming-your-profile) for how to fully confirm it. Already fully confirmed and still stuck? Email support@staffer.com.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If I fix my profile, does it change what a company already saw about me?">
    Only going forward. A company that already reviewed you for a specific application keeps the copy of your profile from that moment. Your edit is what everyone sees from now on, including that same company the next time they look you up.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
