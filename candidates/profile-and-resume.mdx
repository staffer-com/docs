Your profile is a mix of what's publicly known about you and what you've told Staffer directly, and your own word always wins.

## Editing

Open the Resume tab to edit anything: your headline, current role, location, bio, skills, languages. Changes save immediately and are what recruiters see from then on.

## What happens underneath

The public information behind your profile keeps updating over time. None of it overwrites a field you've personally set. Your edits stick until you change them again yourself.

## Editing from chat

You can also ask the copilot to update a specific field, for instance "set my headline to X." It confirms the change before making it. Bigger edits, like your skills list, need the Resume tab directly.
