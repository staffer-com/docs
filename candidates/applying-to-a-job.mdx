You can apply to any open listing on Staffer, with or without a Staffer account. If a recruiter reaches out to you first instead, see [When a recruiter contacts you](/candidates/recruiter-contact).

## Find the listing

Job listings on Staffer live in three places: a company's own career page, Staffer's job board, or a direct link someone sent you. All three lead to the same listing page, with the same two ways to apply.

## Apply

Every open listing has two apply buttons. Pick whichever suits you.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Apply with resume">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click Apply with resume">
        This works whether or not you have a Staffer account.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Upload your CV">
        Drop it in or click to browse. PDF or .docx, up to 10 MB.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check your details">
        Staffer reads your CV and fills in your **Name**, **Email** and an optional **Message** to the hiring team. Anything you've already typed stays as you typed it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Consent and submit">
        Check the box consenting to the company (and Staffer) processing your details for this application. A second, optional box asks if you'd like to be considered for other roles at the company later. Click **Submit application**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Sign in to apply">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click the apply button">
        It reads **Sign in to apply** if you're not signed in. Signing in with Google, Microsoft or LinkedIn creates a Staffer account if you don't have one. If you haven't set up your profile yet, Staffer takes you through that first. See [Sign in](/candidates/signing-in) and [Claim your profile](/candidates/claiming-your-profile).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm consent">
        A dialog asks you to consent to the company processing your profile for this application, plus the same optional box for future roles.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Send your application">
        Staffer sends your existing profile as your application. There's no form to fill in.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What happens after you apply

- If you applied without an account, you'll get an email confirming it, with a private link you can use to follow the application from there. No sign-in needed. If you applied while signed in, it shows up straight away in your [applications list](/candidates/tracking-your-application).
- The company reviews your application. See [Track your applications](/candidates/tracking-your-application) for the stages you'll see.
- You may get steps to complete, like a questionnaire, an AI interview, or booking a time to meet the team, once the company moves you into their process. See [Screening steps](/candidates/screening-steps).

## Applying twice

If you apply again with the same email through **Apply with resume**, Staffer recognizes you and offers **Resend my link** instead of letting you submit a new application. If you're signed in and already applied to a job, you'll see "You already applied to this role" instead of the apply buttons. Applying twice with two different emails, or switching between the two ways of applying, can create two separate applications for the same job. Stick to one method and one email if you want to be sure it stays one application.

## Closed listings

Once a job stops taking applications, its listing shows **Applications closed** instead of the apply buttons. If you'd already applied before it closed, that doesn't change anything for you: your application keeps moving through the company's process as normal.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a Staffer account to apply?">
    No. **Apply with resume** only needs your CV, name, email and consent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What file types can I upload for Apply with resume?">
    PDF or .docx, up to 10 MB.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I applied but don't have a Staffer account. How do I check on it?">
    Use the private link from your confirmation email. It works without signing in. See [When a recruiter contacts you](/candidates/recruiter-contact) for how that link works.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if Staffer reads my CV wrong?">
    Reading your CV is best-effort. If it misses something, fix the fields yourself before you submit. Your original file is always saved and sent to the company either way.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
