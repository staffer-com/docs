Every screen has a chat panel where you can ask questions or get something done, not just browse.

## What it can help with

Ask about a company you're considering, search for jobs, check where you stand on an application, or ask it to compare your resume against similar candidates in the market and suggest what's missing, anonymously, without seeing anyone else's identity.

## What it always confirms first

Withdrawing an application or changing a field on your profile always shows you exactly what it's about to do and waits for you to press confirm, unless you've turned that off yourself.

## Your choice on confirmations

Decide for yourself whether the copilot should always ask before acting, or act right away and just tell you afterward. Change it anytime from the chat panel.

<Info>
  Your conversation with the copilot isn't saved to your account. It clears when you close the tab.
</Info>
