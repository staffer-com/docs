Once you've applied somewhere, or a recruiter has added you to their process, sign in to Staffer and open **Applications** to follow it.

## Your applications list

Each row shows the company and job, its current stage, and a short note on what's next: **Your turn** when something needs you, or **Waiting on team** when it's the company's move. Search by role or company, or filter by stage. Open the **…** menu on a row to view the application, copy its ID, or withdraw.

If the list is empty, you haven't applied anywhere yet and no recruiter has added you. See [Apply to a job](/candidates/applying-to-a-job) or [Find jobs](/candidates/searching-for-jobs) to start.

## Inside an application

Every application has five tabs:

- **Job**: the listing, as posted.
- **Resume**: your profile as the company sees it for this specific application, not necessarily your current one.
- **Process**: where you are and what's next. See below.
- **Messages**: your conversation with the recruiter. See [Messages](/candidates/messages).
- **Privacy**: your consent, your data, and where you withdraw. See [Withdraw an application](/candidates/withdrawing).

## What you see on the Process tab

It's a timeline of every step in the company's process for you. Completed and skipped steps collapse into a **Previous steps** group. The one step that's current is shown in full. Steps after that are marked **Upcoming** and only show their name and kind until it's your turn.

Once everything's done, the tab shows a closing note that the hiring team will take it from here.

## Stages, and what they mean

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| **In review** | The company has your application and hasn't started moving you through their process yet. |
| **In progress** | You're being actively taken through the process: a step is open, or the company is reviewing you. |
| **Offer** | The company has made you an offer. |
| **Hired** | You got the job. |
| **Rejected** | The company decided not to move forward. |
| **Withdrawn** | You pulled out yourself. See [Withdraw an application](/candidates/withdrawing). |

## Why a new application might not show up yet

If a recruiter added you to a job, it may not appear in this list right away. An application you started yourself shows up immediately, at any stage. One a recruiter started for you only appears once they've moved you past their first review stage. This doesn't affect the private link they emailed you: that works from the moment you get it, whether or not the application shows up here yet. See [When a recruiter contacts you](/candidates/recruiter-contact).

## Emails you might get

| When | What it's about |
|---|---|
| A recruiter messages you | The message itself, which you can reply to. |
| You apply without an account | Confirms your application and gives you your private link. |
| A step needs you | Something's ready for you to do: a questionnaire, an upload, an interview. |
| You submit something | Confirms the company received it. |
| An interview is booked | The time, joining details and an agenda. |
| An interview is moved or cancelled | What changed. A cancelled slot isn't a rejection. The time is being released. |
| A decision is made | An update from the company, for example if they're not moving forward. |

You may also get emails about your data and privacy rights. See [Your data and privacy](/candidates/data-and-privacy).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see an application a recruiter told me about?">
    It's probably still at their first review stage. It appears here once they move you forward. Until then, use the private link they sent you. See [When a recruiter contacts you](/candidates/recruiter-contact).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my stage change without anyone reviewing me personally?">
    Usually a person at the company moves you. But a questionnaire can be set up to act on your answer immediately, which can move you forward or end your application without anyone individually reviewing it in the moment. See [Screening steps](/candidates/screening-steps).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does the Resume tab look different from my actual profile?">
    It's a copy of your profile as it was for this specific application, not a live view of your current one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
