Every application shows its actual current stage, not a guess.

## The stages

Shortlisted, in review, in progress, offer, hired, rejected, or withdrawn. A stage only changes because a recruiter moved it or you withdrew yourself, never automatically overnight.

## What's inside an application

Open one to see its screening steps and what's expected of you next, your message thread with the recruiter, and your resume as they see it for this specific role.

## If you get moved backward

It happens, recruiters correct mistakes. It's recorded plainly as a correction, not treated as some new decision made about you.
