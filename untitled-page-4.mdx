One price list, the same on every plan. Your money buys credits one to one, and a commitment comes with extra credits on top.

|  | **Free** | **Growth** | **Enterprise** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Committed usage / mo | $$0 ($$10 free usage) | \$3,000 | Custom |
| Bonus credits on committed amount | None | \+5% | \+10% |
| Unused credits | Roll over | Roll over | Roll over |

**Free** includes AI candidate search, deep scoring, automated outreach, one workspace, and unlimited seats.

**Growth** adds unlimited workspaces, dedicated support, ATS / HRIS sync, and team usage controls.

**Enterprise** adds 10% more usage, priority (P1) support, and SSO with role-based access control.

## How the bonus works

A $$3,000 monthly commitment lands as $$3,150 of credits. The bonus sits on your committed amount only, extra usage beyond it draws at the standard one-to-one rate. See [Top-ups](/untitled-page-3) for adding credit beyond your commitment.

Bonus credits are usage only and can't be withdrawn. Credits never expire, so anything you don't spend in a quiet month carries forward to a busy one.
