Staffer is priced entirely on usage. You pay for the work the platform actually performs on your hiring, and nothing else. There are no seats, no licence fees, no per-user charges, and no fee tied to a candidate signing.

You buy credits, and the platform draws down against them as it works. Committing to usage earns you extra credits on top, so the same budget buys more hiring, not a cheaper rate. The price list itself never changes.

## What usage covers

Every unit of work the platform runs for you:

- AI sourcing across the candidate database
- Match scoring with per-criterion rationale
- Enrichment and contact data
- Agent work and actions
- AI Interviews

A full hire, end to end, typically draws around **\$1,285** in usage. Lighter roles draw less, harder ones more.
