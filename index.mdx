Staffer is a hiring platform for recruiting teams. You describe a job in plain language, and an AI agent drafts the brief, searches for candidates, scores every candidate against your criteria and works out what should happen next with each one. People on your team decide who moves forward and send every message.

Candidates use Staffer too, for free. They follow their applications, complete the steps you set up, reply to you and keep their own profile up to date.

## How a hire moves through Staffer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a job">
    Describe the role to the agent. It drafts the brief: the job details, a role description and the criteria you'll score candidates against, each marked **Required** or **Preferred**. [Create a job](/companies/create-a-job)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find candidates">
    Ask the agent to search Staffer's candidate database, add people yourself, or publish a listing so people can apply. Every candidate gets a match score for this job. [Search for candidates](/companies/running-a-search)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shortlist and promote">
    Shortlist the people worth a closer look, then promote them into the job's pipeline. Promoting sets up their hiring process and a private link to their application. [Shortlist and promote](/companies/shortlist-and-push)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run your process">
    Each job has its own steps: reviews, questionnaires, interviews, AI interviews and more. Staffer shows each candidate the next action and who it's waiting on. [Build a hiring process](/companies/build-a-process)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    Move candidates to **Offer**, **Hired** or **Rejected** when you've made the call. Every change is logged with who made it and when. [Change a candidate's stage](/companies/candidate-state)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the agent does, and what you do

| The agent | You |
|---|---|
| Drafts the brief from your description | Check the criteria and fix what's wrong |
| Runs searches and scores every result | Choose who to shortlist and promote |
| Explains each score, criterion by criterion | Decide what the evidence means |
| Works out the next action for each candidate | Send the message or move the stage |
| Drafts the job listing | Publish it |
| Runs AI interviews when you add one to the process | Review the transcript and decide |

In **Ask permission** mode, the agent asks before it changes anything that matters, such as shortlisting people or editing the process. See [What the agent can do](/companies/agent-actions).

## What Staffer doesn't do

- It doesn't write and send messages to candidates on its own. A person on your team sends every message. The only automatic emails are the notices your process sends, such as a link to a questionnaire when it's the candidate's turn.
- It doesn't reject or advance anyone because of a score. Candidates are only rejected automatically if you set up a step to do that, such as a questionnaire with a qualifying question.
- It doesn't import candidates from another applicant tracking system, and it doesn't post your jobs to LinkedIn or Indeed. Listings go on your Staffer career page and the public Staffer job board.
