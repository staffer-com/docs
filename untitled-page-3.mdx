Need more than your committed amount covers this month? Top up any time.

## How it works

Top-ups draw credit one to one with what you pay, the same price list as everything else. There's no minimum, no penalty, and no approval step. Add credit and it's available immediately.

<Note>
  The bonus on your plan applies only to your committed amount. A top-up beyond it is straight credit, no bonus attached.
</Note>

## If you're topping up often

That's usually a sign a higher commitment is the cheaper route: the bonus on a larger committed amount is worth more than what you'd get from repeated one-off top-ups. Move to a higher commitment any time from Settings.

## Where top-up credit goes

Paid credits and bonus credits are tracked separately but spend from the same balance. Nothing you top up expires, so a quiet month still leaves it there for a busy one.
