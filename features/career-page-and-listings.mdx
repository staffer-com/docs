A listing isn't something you write from scratch. It's generated from the job you already built, and stays in sync with it.

## Generated from the brief, not a blank page

Ask for a listing and the agent writes the title, tagline, body, and apply button from your job's hard fields and criteria. Ask for changes and it edits the existing copy instead of starting over.

## Your own career page

Every workspace gets a public page listing its open roles, branded with your logo, colors, and office locations, not a generic template.

## Go anonymous when you need to

For a confidential search, hide your company's name completely. Staffer generates a genuinely separate version of the listing body written without your identity in it, and refuses to publish it if it would still give you away.
