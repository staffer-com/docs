A job's **Brief** tab has four parts: **Details** (structured fields), **Description** (free text), **Required** and **Preferred** criteria (what Staffer actually scores candidates against), and **Notes** (private context for the agent). On a wide screen, **Details** sits in a column on the right.

## Details

The first rows organize the job. They appear once the job has been saved:

| Field | What you set |
|---|---|
| **Status** | **Draft**, **Planned**, **Qualifying**, **Open** or **Closed**. Picking **Closed** opens the **Close position** dialog. See [Job statuses](/companies/projects#job-statuses). |
| **Project** | The [project](/companies/projects) this job belongs to, or **No project**. The arrow next to it opens the project. |
| **Team** | One of your workspace's [teams](/companies/teams), or **No team**. The agent then also uses that team's knowledge on this job. |
| **Members** | A roster of people on the job. It's informational only: it doesn't grant or restrict access. |
| **Openings** | How many people you're hiring, shown as "N to hire". 1 by default. Turn on **Close when filled** to close the job once every opening is hired. See [Close, reopen or delete a job](/companies/close-reopen-delete#when-every-opening-is-hired). |

The rest describe the job itself:

| Field | What you set |
|---|---|
| **Function** | One of Engineering, Design, Product, Data, Security, Marketing, Sales, Operations, Other. |
| **Engagement** | Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Freelance, Project, or Internship. Defaults to Full-time, and changes what the Salary field asks for. |
| **Work mode** | On-site, Hybrid, Remote, or Unspecified. |
| **Location** | One place. The field only ever holds a single location. |
| **Languages** | Any number, each with a star to mark one **primary**. The primary language is the one your listing is written in first. |
| **Salary** | A range, or for a project engagement, one fixed amount (the row is then called **Fixed price**), plus a currency: EUR, GBP, USD, CAD, CHF, SEK, NOK, DKK, PLN, CZK, HUF, RON, BGN, ISK or MXN. Freelance jobs also ask whether the rate is hourly or monthly, and the row is called **Hourly rate** or **Monthly rate**. A **Hide on public listing** toggle keeps the number visible to your team while hiding it from candidates. |
| **Timezone overlap** | Minimum hours of overlap you need, shown as "≥ Nh". |
| **Relocation** | Unspecified, Yes, No, or Considered. |
| **Start target** | Free text, not a date picker, for example "Q3", "ASAP", or "2026-09". |
| **Deadline** | A date picker. You can't pick a day before today. Leave it empty if the job has no deadline. |

## Description

A free-text field for the role, team, and what success looks like. This is prose, separate from your criteria, and it's what a reader sees before the bullet points.

## Required and Preferred criteria

Each criterion is one plain sentence, sorted into **Required** or **Preferred**. Required criteria count double compared with preferred ones when Staffer works out a candidate's [match score](/companies/match-scores). For how to phrase them so they score well, see [Write criteria that score well](/academy/write-criteria-that-score-well).

Type a criterion into the row at the bottom of either section and press Enter to add it. The row clears so you can add another. Each criterion can be up to 400 characters, and a brief holds 30 across both sections combined. Once you hit that, the add row disappears until you remove one.

There's no drag-to-reorder, and no way to switch a criterion from Preferred to Required (or back) in place. To move one, delete it and retype it in the other section. The retyped criterion counts as a new one, so candidates' earlier scores for the old wording don't carry over to it.

## Notes

A free-text field for anything you want the agent to keep in mind that shouldn't appear anywhere candidate-facing: internal constraints, hiring-manager preferences, budget context. It holds up to 4,000 characters, and only appears once the job has been saved at least once.

Anyone in your workspace who opens the job can read Notes. They're never shown to candidates, never on the public listing, and never included in a share link. The agent does read them, including when it decides how to search for candidates, but Notes are never used to score a candidate and never copied into the brief, criteria, or listing.

## Saving

Edits to Details, Description, or criteria save when you click **Save**, at the top right of the tab. It shows **Saving** while it's in flight, and **Saved** once it lands. If you close the page before saving, your edits are still there when you come back.

Closing a job doesn't lock its brief. You can still correct it, for example before you reopen the job. **Status**, **Project**, **Team** and **Members** save as soon as you change them, without **Save**. **Openings** and **Close when filled** save with the rest of the brief.

## When the agent edits while you work

The agent can update the brief while you're working on it. If it saves a change while you have unsaved edits, Staffer keeps your version on screen and shows that a newer version exists. It asks you once whether to keep your edits or see the update. Your edits aren't merged with the agent's, so you choose one version or the other. The same applies to the job's listing. The process works differently: the agent edits your unsaved draft instead. See [Build a hiring process](/companies/build-a-process).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I reorder my criteria?">
    No. New ones always go to the end of their section. If the order matters to you, write them in the order you want from the start.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I meant to mark a criterion Required, not Preferred. How do I fix it?">
    Delete it from Preferred and retype it under Required. There's no in-place switch, and the retyped version is a new criterion with no history of its own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do Notes affect a candidate's match score?">
    No. Notes steer how the agent talks about the role and how it searches. Only the brief's criteria feed into scoring.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
