The **Roles** page lists every job in your workspace that you can see. Admins and owners see them all. Everyone else sees the jobs they created or are on the team for. See [Who can see a job](/get-started/roles-and-permissions#who-can-see-a-job). Every job also carries the same **More role actions** menu, whether you open it from a list row or from inside the job itself.

## The Roles page

The job table takes the whole page. To ask the agent about your jobs, click **Agent** at the bottom right. See [Work with the agent](/companies/the-agent).

- **New role**, at the top right, adds a job by title without the agent and opens its brief. See [Create a job](/companies/create-a-job).
- **All roles** shows every job you can see. **My roles** shows only jobs you created or are a member of.
- The search icon opens **Search roles…**, which matches job names only.
- **Filter** adds conditions on **Status**, **Function**, **Engagement**, and **Location**. Values within one filter combine as "or", and different filters combine as "and".
- **Display options** covers **Sort by** (16 options, including Updated, Name, Deadline, Function, Version, Created, and Candidates, each ascending or descending) and **Columns**. Name always shows. Function, Candidates, Deadline, Updated, Listing and Status show by default. Engagement, Location, Version and Created are available but off by default.

<Note>
  The **Status** filter and the **Status** column aren't the same thing. The filter works on the listing: **Published**, **Draft**, **Closed** or **Not listed**. The column shows the job's own status: **Draft**, **Planned**, **Qualifying**, **Open** or **Closed**. Only **Qualifying** and **Open** jobs take candidates, and a job can be open with no listing at all. To group jobs by status, sort by **Status** instead. See [Job statuses](/companies/projects#job-statuses).
</Note>

**Candidates** shows a live count of everyone on the job. The table paginates at 50 rows by default. Your search text resets each visit, but filters, sort, and column choices are remembered, per browser, not per account: they won't follow you to a different computer or show up for a teammate.

## The job header and its actions menu

Inside a job, the header shows the job's name, its status and the same **More role actions** (**…**) menu as the list. Click the job's name to open **Switch to role…** and jump to another job.

**Members** and **Team** aren't in this menu. They're rows in the **Details** section of the job's **Brief** tab. **Members** is the job's team: apart from admins and owners, only the people listed there and whoever created the job can see it. **Team** assigns the job to one of your workspace's teams, and the agent then also uses that team's knowledge on this job. **Team** doesn't give anyone access.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Open role** | Opens the job. Only in the menu on a row of the **Roles** page. |
| **Notify me** | Toggles your own notifications for this job: a candidate replies, a new candidate lands on the job, an interview finishes, or any other process step completes. The job's creator is subscribed automatically. Everyone else opts in here. |
| **Share…** | Creates a public, read-only link to one or more candidates on the job. See [Share candidates](/companies/share-candidates). |
| **Duplicate role** | Makes an exact copy immediately, no confirmation: the brief, criteria, listing (as an unpublished draft), and process, but not candidates or notes. The copy always opens **Open**, even from a closed job. See [Create a job](/companies/create-a-job) for how this differs from asking the agent to start a job from an existing one. |
| **Move to workspace** | Moves the job into a different workspace in the same organization. Only appears if you're an admin or owner of both workspaces. It's a move, not a copy: the job leaves the workspace you moved it from. |
| **Copy role ID** | Copies the job's internal ID. |
| **Close position** / **Reopen position** | Stops or resumes new candidates and applications. See [Close, reopen or delete a job](/companies/close-reopen-delete). |
| **Delete role** | Hides the job everywhere in the app and unpublishes its listing. See [Close, reopen or delete a job](/companies/close-reopen-delete). |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I filter for open jobs?">
    The **Status** filter works on the listing (**Published**, **Draft**, **Closed**, **Not listed**), not on the job's own status. Sort by **Status** to group jobs by status, or look at the **Status** column. "Not listed" only means the job has no published listing: the position itself can still be open.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does adding someone as a Member change whether they can see the job?">
    Yes. Members, reviewers and viewers only see a job if they're in its **Members** row, created it, or were assigned a step on it. What they can do on it still depends on their workspace role. Admins and owners see every job either way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I changed my columns and sort order. Why don't they show up on my other laptop?">
    View preferences are saved to your browser, not your account. Set them again on any other device.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
