The jobs list shows every job in your workspace. A job appears here once the agent opens its first draft. See [Create a job](/companies/create-a-job).

## Job status

Every job is **Open** or **Closed**, and the status shows on the list. Filter by status to see only the jobs you're actively hiring for, or sort by it to group them.

## The job header

When you open a job, a header at the top of the canvas shows which job you're in. It stays in view while you work on the brief, listing, process, and candidates.
