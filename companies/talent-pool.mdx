Your talent pool is everyone your workspace has shortlisted, promoted or received an application from, on any job. It also includes anyone you saved with **Save to talent pool**, even if they've never been on a job. You can save someone from their **Candidate actions** menu or from a search result.

## Columns

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Name | Avatar, name, a small **Claimed** badge if they've claimed their own profile, and their title at company (or "Unknown role" if neither is on file). |
| Applications | How many jobs they've been part of. |
| Last activity | The most recent activity from either side, yours or theirs. Shows a dash if there's none. |
| Active / Inactive | Whether they're currently in a live pipeline. See below. |
| Engagement | **Warm**, **Lukewarm**, **Cold**, or **No activity**, based on the candidate's own recent activity. See below. |

Applications, Last activity, and Engagement hide when the window is narrow. Name and the Active/Inactive badge always show.

**Active** means at least one of their applications is on an open job, at a stage other than **Rejected**, **Hired** or **Withdrawn**. Everyone else shows **Inactive**, including people you saved who aren't on any job. Hover the badge to see why.

**Engagement** only counts what the candidate did: replying to a message, updating their consent or completing a step such as a questionnaire. Nothing your team does changes it. **Warm** means within the last 7 days, **Lukewarm** within 30, **Cold** past that, and **No activity** means never.

Active/Inactive and Engagement answer different questions. A candidate can be **Active** with no recent activity of their own (they're sitting **In review** on an open job), or **Inactive** but still **Warm** (they replied days ago on a job that's since closed).

## Search, filter, and sort

**Search the pool…** matches by name. **Filter** has two options: **Status** (Active or Inactive) and **Engagement**. **Display options** only sets the sort order. You can't pick columns here. The sort options are **Most recent activity** (default), **Most engaged first**, **Most applications**, and **Name · A → Z**.

## Add a candidate

**Add candidate** searches people already in Staffer's candidate database. Type a name and pick a result. People already in your pool don't show up, and blocked profiles show a **Blocked** label instead of an add option. Only members, admins and owners see this button.

## Row actions

Each row's **⋯** menu offers **View profile**, **Copy ID**, and, for members and above, **Save to talent pool** (or **Remove from talent pool** if they're already saved).

## Opening a profile

Click a row to open the person's page. It has two tabs, **Applications** and **Activity**, and a profile panel you can hide.

- **Applications** lists every job they've been part of. Click a row to open them in that job. Someone you saved who isn't on a job shows "hasn't applied to any of your jobs".
- **Activity** is a timeline of what's happened with them.

## Ask anything

The **Ask anything** box on this page is a chat with the agent about your talent pool. Ask it to filter the table ("show only warm candidates") or open a profile. The conversation isn't saved: reload the page and it's gone.

## No match scores here

Scores belong to a job, so the talent pool doesn't show them. Open a job's **Candidates** or **Scouting** tab to see how someone matches that job. See [How match scores work](/companies/match-scores).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does shortlisting someone put them in my talent pool?">
    Yes. Shortlisting, promoting and applying all add someone, as well as **Save to talent pool**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see the Engagement or Applications columns?">
    They hide when your browser window is narrow. Widen the window to see them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can add someone to the talent pool?">
    Members, admins and owners. Viewers and reviewers can see the pool but can't add or remove anyone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Active and Warm?">
    **Active** means they're in a live pipeline now. **Warm** means they did something themselves in the last 7 days, like replying to a message. Someone can be one without the other.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
