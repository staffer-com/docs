---
title: "Shortlist and promote candidates"
description: "Save candidates to a job's shortlist without committing to them, then promote the ones you want into your pipeline."
icon: "bookmark"
---

Shortlisting saves a candidate to one job without putting them in your process. Promoting moves them in. Both need the member role or higher in the workspace.

## Shortlist a candidate

- Click the bookmark icon on a row in the **Scouting** tab, or choose **Shortlist** from its **Candidate actions** (…) menu.
- Open a search result and click **+ Shortlist** on its profile panel.
- Select several rows with the checkboxes, then click **Shortlist** in the bar that appears.
- Ask the agent in the job's chat: "shortlist the top 5." In **Ask permission** mode, it confirms first.

## Where shortlisted candidates live

In the **Shortlist** group at the top of the job's **Scouting** tab, and in your workspace's [Talent pool](/companies/talent-pool). They don't appear on the **Candidates** tab, and they don't count as candidates, until you promote them.

## Remove someone from the shortlist

Click the filled bookmark again, or choose **Remove from shortlist** from the row menu. There's no confirmation. You can shortlist them again at any time. Removing someone doesn't block them.

## Promote candidates into your pipeline

- **Promote**, on the **Shortlist** group's header, promotes the whole shortlist at once. There's no partial option from this button. It's disabled with a tooltip, **Shortlist someone first** or **Position is closed**, when there's nobody to promote or the job's closed.
- **Promote to candidate**, from a row's **Candidate actions** menu or from the bar that appears once you select rows, promotes one or more specific people.
- In chat, ask the agent to promote people. In **Ask permission** mode it confirms first: for the whole shortlist, "I'd like to promote everyone on the shortlist into the Candidates pipeline. Heads-up: this freezes an application snapshot for each — irreversible for the candidate." For a subset, "I'd like to promote N candidate(s) into the Candidates pipeline."

## What happens when you promote someone

<Warning>
Promoting never emails the candidate. It's silent. The candidate's **Process** tab immediately recommends **Send intro outreach** as the next step, but nothing goes out until you or a teammate actually sends it.
</Warning>

For each candidate promoted:

1. Staffer saves a copy of their profile as it is now, for this application. Later changes to their live profile don't change what you reviewed.
2. A private application link is created for them.
3. The job's process steps are set up for their candidacy.
4. They move to **In review**.

## If a promotion fails

If Staffer can't promote someone, for example because they're blocked in your workspace, they stay on the shortlist. The message shows a count, not names: "N candidate(s) could not be promoted and stayed in the shortlist". Check the **Shortlist** group to see who's left. A blocked candidate shows a **Blocked** label.

## Closed jobs

A closed job refuses new candidates entirely: "This position is closed and takes no new candidates." Reopen it first. See [Close, reopen or delete a job](/companies/close-reopen-delete).

## No confirmation dialog

**Promote** and **Promote to candidate** act as soon as you click. There's no confirmation dialog. Only the agent asks first, and only in **Ask permission** mode.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is a shortlisted candidate the same as a candidate?">
    No. The shortlist is a holding area on one job's **Scouting** tab. People become candidates, and show up on the **Candidates** tab, once you promote them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does promoting send the candidate anything?">
    No. Promoting saves a copy of their profile, creates their application link and sets up their process steps, but sends nothing. You send the first message yourself, from the next action or the **Messages** tab.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I promote someone straight from a search, skipping the shortlist?">
    Yes. You don't have to shortlist first. Use **Promote to candidate** in a row's **Candidate actions** menu or the selection bar, or ask the agent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I promote the whole shortlist and one person fails?">
    Everyone else moves to **Candidates**. Anyone who couldn't be promoted stays on the shortlist and is counted in the message.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
