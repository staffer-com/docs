Everything in Staffer hangs off one sidebar, always visible on the left unless you collapse it.

## The sidebar

The sidebar separates what's yours from what belongs to the workspace you're in.

The top group is yours. **Notifications**, **My applications** and **Calendar** cover every workspace you belong to:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Notifications** | Bell icon with an unread-count badge. Opens a popover of recent notifications. See [Notifications](/companies/notifications). |
| **My applications** | Every application you're involved in, across all your workspaces. See [Candidate lists](/companies/candidates-list). |
| **Calendar** | Your scheduled interviews and connected calendars. See [Calendar and availability](/companies/calendar). |
| **Agent** | Opens a new chat with the agent, full screen. Describe a job there to start one. See [Create a job](/companies/create-a-job). |

The **Workspace** group belongs to the workspace you're in. Click **Workspace** to fold it away:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Projects** | Groups of related jobs, each with its own status, team and dates. See [Group jobs into projects](/companies/projects). |
| **Roles** | Your workspace's full list of jobs. See [Manage your jobs](/companies/jobs-list). |
| **Applications** | Every candidate across every job, with its own unread badge. |
| **Messages** | Your message threads with candidates, with an unread badge. |
| **Talent pool** | Everyone your workspace has shortlisted, promoted, received an application from or saved, across all jobs. |
| **Segments** | Saved groups of people from your talent pool. See [Segments](/companies/segments). |
| **Analytics** | Workspace-wide hiring stats. |

At the bottom of the sidebar sit **MCP**, marked **Beta**, and **Support**.

Below the nav sits **Recents**: a collapsible list of jobs you've opened recently, up to 12. Hover over a job and click the pin icon to keep it above Recents. Each job shows a colored dot: green means its listing is live, grey means the listing is a draft or closed. Hovering the dot spells it out: "listing is live," "listing is a draft," or "listing closed."

Click the collapse icon at the top of the sidebar to shrink it to icons only. This is saved for your browser.

## The agent bar

A bar along the bottom of every page holds the agent. At the right end, **Agent** opens the chat in the corner, over the page you're on, and the clock icon next to it opens your chat history. Your latest chats sit as tabs just left of **Agent**, up to four. See [Work with the agent](/companies/the-agent).

## Links open in the right workspace

Every link in Staffer includes its workspace. A link from an email or a teammate opens in the right workspace, even if you last worked in another one. Older links still work.

## Search

Click the search icon at the top of the sidebar, or press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows), from anywhere. Type at least 2 characters to search candidates, jobs, teams, members, and every page you have access to. Results are grouped by type: **Pages**, **Candidates**, **Roles**, **Teams** and **Members**. Pick one to go straight there.

## Notifications, messages, and usage

The **Notifications**, **Applications**, and **Messages** sidebar items each carry their own unread-count badge, updated roughly once a minute. Opening the notifications popover shows **Mark all read** and a link to see everything on the full notifications page.

**Usage**, in the workspace menu, shows either your organization's available credit balance, or, if your workspace has a monthly spend cap set, how much of that cap you've used this month. Click it to open your usage details, if you have access to them.

## MCP

**MCP**, marked **Beta**, at the bottom of the sidebar opens **Connected apps**, where you connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other assistants. See [Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor](/companies/connect-an-assistant).

## Support

Click **Support** at the bottom of the sidebar to open a chat window for help. It's the only way to reach support from inside the app: there's no floating help bubble anywhere else.

## The workspace menu

Click your workspace name at the bottom of the sidebar to open:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Switch workspace** | Search or pick from every workspace you belong to. Press a number key from **1** to **9** (when you're not typing in a field) to jump straight to that workspace in the list. |
| **Usage** | Your credit balance, or this month's spend against your workspace's cap. |
| **Settings** | Your account and workspace settings. |
| **Invite members** | Opens **Settings** → **Workspace** → **Members**, where you click **Invite teammate**. |
| **Theme** | System, Light, or Dark. |
| **Log out** | Signs you out of Staffer entirely. |

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Does this | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** | Opens search | Anywhere |
| **1**-**9** | Jumps to that workspace | Anywhere, when not typing |
| **J** / **K** | Next / previous candidate | A candidate's own page |
| Hold **⌘D** / **Ctrl+D** | Push-to-talk voice input | Any chat composer |
| **Enter** / **Shift+Enter** | Send message / add a line break | Any chat composer |

Only the search and microphone buttons mention their shortcuts in a tooltip.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why don't my teammates see the same Recents or pinned jobs I do?">
    Pinned jobs and Recents are stored in your browser, not on your account. They don't sync across your own devices either, and nobody else in the workspace sees your list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Search didn't find a candidate I know exists. Why?">
    Search needs at least 2 characters, and only returns candidates, jobs, teams, and members, plus settings pages you have permission to open. It won't surface something you don't have access to.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
