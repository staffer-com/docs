**Analytics** shows how your whole workspace is hiring: how fast, where candidates drop off and where they come from.

- Every number covers the whole workspace, for all time. You can't filter by date or job.
- Everyone in the workspace can see it, including viewers.

## The four numbers at the top

| Card | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Avg time to hire** | Time from a candidate joining a job to being moved to **Hired**. |
| **Avg candidates per position** | Mean across every job in the workspace, including roles with zero applicants. |
| **Avg time on next action** | Time from a process step starting to it being completed. |
| **Hires** | Candidates currently at **Hired**. |

Things to know:

- **Hires** counts who's hired right now, not everyone ever hired. If a hired candidate later moves to a different stage, they drop out of the count.
- **Avg candidates per position** includes closed jobs, and jobs with no candidates count as zero, which pulls the average down.
- The three time numbers show a dash until there's enough data.

## Pipeline flow

A flow chart of how candidates move between stages, including drop-offs to **Rejected** and **Withdrawn**. It only draws stages someone has moved into, so a workspace with no hires yet has no **Offer** or **Hired**. Moves back to an earlier stage aren't drawn. A footnote below the chart counts them.

The chart calls the **In progress** stage "In process", and so do the time-at-stage tiles below. It's the same stage.

## Where candidates come from

**Source attribution** splits candidates into **Applied** (they applied to a listing), **Sourced** (found in a search) and **Manually added**, with a count and a percentage for each.

## Candidates by function

Candidates grouped by the job's **Function**. Jobs with no function set show under **Unspecified**.

## Time at each stage

The average time candidates spend in **In review**, **In process** (In progress) and **Offer** before moving on. A tile shows **No data** until someone has left that stage.

## Jobs active per day

How many jobs your workspace had on each of the last 30 days. Closed and unpublished jobs count too. Only deleted jobs don't.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I filter analytics by date range or by job?">
    No. Everything covers the whole workspace, for all time. For one job, use its **Candidates** tab.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does Pipeline flow say In process instead of In progress?">
    It's the same stage. Only the Analytics page uses the label "In process".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Avg candidates per position lower than I expected?">
    It averages across every job in the workspace, including closed jobs and jobs with no candidates.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
