Staffer suggests one next action for each candidate. It works it out from their stage, their messages, their process steps and how they compare with others in the same job.

- It's worked out again every time you look, so it always matches what has happened.
- Nothing sends or changes until you act on it. Acting on it needs the member role or higher. Reviewers and viewers can only complete steps they're named on.
- You can't dismiss or snooze it. It changes when you act on it or when something happens with the candidate.

## Where you see it

- The **Process** tab's **Next step** card, marked with a purple **AI recommendation** badge, often with a composer or editor already open underneath it.
- The **Next action** column, filter and group-by option on candidate lists. See [Candidate lists](/companies/candidates-list).
- **Speed action**, for working through several at once (below).

## Every action

### In review

| Title | Appears when | Acting on it |
|---|---|---|
| **Acknowledge application** | They applied and you haven't responded | Opens a composer pre-filled with an acknowledgement message. Sending moves them to In progress. |
| **Send intro outreach** | You sourced them and haven't reached out yet | Opens a composer pre-filled with an outreach message. |
| **Send follow-up** | You've messaged, no reply for 5 or more days | Opens a composer pre-filled with a follow-up message. |
| **Wait for reply** | You've messaged, no reply, under 5 days | Nothing to act on. |
| **Advance to In progress** | They replied | Opens a stage-change control, no message. |

### In progress

| Title | Appears when | Acting on it |
|---|---|---|
| **Advance to Offer** | Every step is done and they're near the top compared with others in this job | Opens a composer pre-filled with the offer message. Sending moves them to Offer. |
| **Reject** | Every step is done and they're near the bottom compared with others in this job | Opens a composer pre-filled with the rejection message. Sending moves them to Rejected. |
| **Wait for the pipeline** | Every step is done, but they're in the middle | Nothing to act on. |
| **Send status check** | Steps still open, no contact for 7 or more days | Opens a composer pre-filled with a follow-up message. |
| **In progress** | Nothing else to flag | Nothing to act on. |

### Offer, Hired, Rejected, Withdrawn

| Title | Appears when | Acting on it |
|---|---|---|
| **Send the offer** | Moved to Offer, never messaged | Opens a composer pre-filled with the offer message. |
| **Follow up on offer** | Offered, no contact for 3 or more days | Opens a composer pre-filled with a follow-up message. |
| **Awaiting decision** | Offered, recently followed up | Nothing to act on. |
| **Send welcome message** | Hired, never welcomed | Opens a composer pre-filled with a welcome message. |
| **Hired** | Hired and welcomed | Nothing to act on. |
| **Send rejection note** | Rejected, never notified | Opens a composer pre-filled with the rejection message. |
| **No next step** | Rejected and notified, or withdrawn | Nothing to act on. |

### Any active stage

| Title | Appears when | Acting on it |
|---|---|---|
| **Reply to their message** | They messaged you and you haven't replied | Opens a composer pre-filled with an AI-drafted reply. |
| **Reject** | A completed interview or questionnaire recommends rejecting, or a completed step scored below its threshold | Opens a composer pre-filled with the rejection message. |
| **Advance to In progress** / **Advance to Offer** | A completed interview or questionnaire recommends advancing | Opens a composer (if advancing to Offer) or a stage-change control. |
| The step's own title, or **Send message** | The next open step is a message step assigned to you | Opens a composer seeded with the step's own message. Sending also completes the step. |
| The step's own title or kind | The next open step is any other kind, assigned to you | Opens that step's editor. |
| **Waiting on `<name>`** | The next open step is assigned to someone else | Nothing to act on. Disabled. |

## Urgency

Every next action has a priority: low, medium, high or critical. It shows with the same bars and colors as on [candidate lists](/companies/candidates-list). The most time-sensitive situations, such as an unanswered message after 3 or more days, or an unsent offer, show as critical.

<Note>
The badge says **AI recommendation**, but fixed rules pick the action, based on the stage, what has happened and how the candidate compares with others in the job. AI only writes the draft reply for **Reply to their message**.
</Note>

## Speed action: work through several at once

Click **Speed action** on a job's **Candidates** tab or the **Applications** page. The table turns into a list of cards, one per candidate, all selected. A card with a message shows the draft collapsed. Expand it to edit before sending. **Skip** hides a card until you leave the page. When you're ready, click **Apply N action(s)** at the bottom.

Only these can be applied in bulk: **Reply to their message**, **Send intro outreach**, process **Send message** steps, **Acknowledge application**, **Send follow-up**, **Send offer**, **Send rejection note**, **Send welcome message**, and plain stage changes. Other actions, such as completing a step, need you to open the candidate.

<Warning>
**Apply N action(s)** fires immediately, with no confirmation. A card you never expanded still sends its seeded message exactly as written. Check anything you haven't opened before you click Apply.
</Warning>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I dismiss or snooze a recommendation?">
    No. **Skip** in Speed action hides a card until you leave the page. Otherwise, a next action only changes when you act on it or when something happens with the candidate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did the recommendation change since I last looked?">
    Staffer works it out again from the candidate's current stage, messages and steps every time you open it. A reply, a completed step or a few days without contact can all change it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Staffer decide on its own to reject or advance someone?">
    No. It only suggests. You send the message or change the stage. The one exception is a questionnaire step with **Automatically reject** turned on, which you set up yourself. See [Assignees, holds and automatic rejection](/companies/reviewer-holds-and-gates).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="If I bulk-apply without expanding every card, what actually gets sent?">
    The pre-filled draft, with that candidate's name and job filled in.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
