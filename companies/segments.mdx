Segments group your talent pool into named, colored groups, such as "Store managers, Oslo" or "Graduate engineers 2027". A candidate can be in as many segments as you like.

## Add candidates to a segment

There are two ways, and you can use both on the same segment:

- **By hand.** Add the people you pick.
- **With Intake.** Set a segment's **Intake** to one or more jobs. Everyone moved to **Qualified** on those jobs joins the segment automatically, including people who qualified before you set it up.

**Qualified** is a stage on every job. Use it for strong candidates you want to keep, for example the runners-up when someone else is hired. See [Change a candidate's stage](/companies/candidate-state).

## The Segments page

The **Segments** page lists every segment in your workspace. For each one you see:

- How many people are in it
- How many joined in the last 30 days
- When its last activity was
- Its engagement

Engagement is the share of a segment's members who did something themselves in the last 30 days, like replying to a message or completing a step. Nothing your team does counts toward it. It's the same idea as **Engagement** in the [talent pool](/companies/talent-pool), measured for the whole segment.

## A segment's own page

Click a segment to open its page, with an overview and the candidates in it.

In the talent pool, each candidate's segments show on their row. See [Talent pool](/companies/talent-pool).

## What segments don't do

A segment doesn't message anyone, and there's no way to send one message to a whole segment. It keeps the right people together so you can find them when a new job opens.
