You can invite teammates from onboarding, or any time afterward from Settings. Both send an email invite. Only Settings offers all four roles.

## Invite during onboarding

The onboarding **Invite your team** screen starts with two empty rows. Add an email and a role for each, up to 20 at once, or click **Skip for now** and invite people later. This screen only offers **Member** or **Admin**. For **Reviewer** or **Viewer**, invite from Settings instead.

## Invite from Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    Go to **Settings** → **Workspace** → **Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start an invite">
    Click **Invite teammate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their email and role">
    Fill in **Work email** and choose a **Role**, then click **Send invite**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The four roles, exactly as the dialog labels them:

| Role | Label in the dialog |
|---|---|
| Admin | Admin — manage workspace + members |
| Member | Member — full app access (default) |
| Reviewer | Reviewer — read-only + complete assigned steps |
| Viewer | Viewer — read-only |

See [Roles and permissions](/get-started/roles-and-permissions) for what each role can do.

## What happens next

The invitee gets an email with an **Accept invite** button. Accepting signs them in (or has them sign in first) and adds them to the workspace with the role you chose. See [Join your company on Staffer](/companies/join-your-team) for what they see, including the wrong-account and expired-link screens.

Invites expire 14 days after they're sent. Staffer refuses to send one to a personal email address or to someone already in the workspace. Inviting an address that already has a pending invite doesn't create a second one: it replaces it, with the 14-day clock restarting from the new invite.

## Resend or revoke

In **Settings** → **Workspace** → **Members**, the **Pending invitations** list shows everyone who hasn't accepted yet. Click **Resend** to send the same invite again and extend its expiry by 14 days. Click the **×** next to their name to revoke it before they accept.

## Who can invite

Only workspace owners and admins can send, resend or revoke invites, whether from onboarding or from Settings.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I invite someone as a Viewer or Reviewer from onboarding?">
    No. The onboarding invite screen only offers Member and Admin. Invite them from **Settings** → **Workspace** → **Members** instead, where all four roles are available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I invited someone twice by mistake. Did they get two emails?">
    They got two emails, but only the latest link works. A second invite to the same address replaces the pending one instead of adding another.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does resending an invite create a new link?">
    No. Resend sends the same link again and extends how long it's valid.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
