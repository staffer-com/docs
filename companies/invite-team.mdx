Step 3 of 3, and the last step of setup. Add anyone who hires with you. You can skip this and do it later from Settings.

## Sending invites

Add up to 20 people at once by email, and set each as a **member** or an **admin**. Each person gets an email with a link to join your workspace.

## Accepting an invite

The link is tied to the exact email address it was sent to:

- Signing in with a different account shows a "wrong account" message and offers to sign out and retry.
- Following a link that's already been used, or one that's expired, says so instead of silently failing.

Invites expire after 14 days. If one lapses, resend it from **Settings → Members** rather than sending a new one from scratch.

<Tip>
  Anyone can be invited at any time, not just during setup. This page works the same way from Settings after you've finished onboarding.
</Tip>
