An organization can contain more than one workspace, for instance separate brands or business units that hire independently but share billing and compliance.

## Organization profile and auto-join

Your organization's own name and details, plus whether new sign-ups on your email domain join an existing workspace automatically. See [Sign in](/companies/sign-in) for what that looks like for the person signing up.

## Every workspace in your organization

A list of every workspace under the organization, so an admin can see the whole picture rather than just their own corner of it.

<Info>
  Organization settings are visible only to organization admins, a separate, broader role from a workspace admin.
</Info>
