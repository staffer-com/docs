Staffer signs you in with your existing Google or Microsoft account. There's no separate password to create or remember.

## You need a work email

Sign-in only accepts a work email domain. Personal providers like Gmail or Outlook.com are rejected, with a prompt to sign in again using your company address instead.

This isn't arbitrary: Staffer organizes everything around your company's workspace, and a personal address has no company to attach to.

## If your company is already on Staffer

<Note>
  If an admin at your company has turned on auto-join for your email domain, signing in drops you straight into your company's existing workspace. You won't see any of the setup steps below: you land directly in the app.
</Note>

If your company has a Staffer organization but auto-join isn't turned on, you can't create a second one on the same domain. You'll be asked to get an invite from an admin there instead. See [Invite your team](/companies/invite-team) for what that invite looks like from the other side.

## Coming back later

Once you're signed in, Staffer keeps track of where you were headed. If you followed a shared link while signed out, sign-in takes you there and then sends you straight back to it after.
