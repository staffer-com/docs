An AI interview step lets a candidate talk to an AI interviewer by chat, voice or video, whenever suits them. You get a transcript and an evaluation. For tips on writing the instructions, see [AI interview playbook](/academy/ai-interview-playbook).

## Set up the step

Open the step from the job's **Process** tab. Its **Setup** tab has:

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| Step name | Shown on the canvas and in the candidate's step history. Defaults to "AI interview". |
| Instructions for the candidate | Shown before the interview starts. This is where you set expectations: length, topics, tone. |
| Instructions for the AI | Only the AI reads this: persona, what to cover, how to follow up. The candidate never sees it. |
| Method | **Chat** (default), **Voice** or **Video**. |
| Language | **English (US)** or **Norwegian**. |
| Voice | Voice and Video only. Nine stock voices, six English and three Norwegian, each with a one-line description. Defaults to Lauren. Press play next to the picker to preview one before choosing it. |
| Speaking speed | Voice and Video only. From 0.7× to 1.2×. Defaults to 1.00×, normal pace. |
| Max length (minutes) | From 5 to 60. Defaults to 15. |
| Interviewer tone | **Warm** (curious, encouraging, default), **Neutral** (professional, structured) or **Rigorous** (probing follow-ups). |
| Reviewers | Optional. Who you expect to look at the results. The step completes when the candidate finishes, whether or not anyone is named. |

Max length doesn't cut the candidate off mid-answer. A couple of minutes before the limit, the AI starts wrapping up. At the limit, it asks one last question and closes. The candidate then sees a short rating screen.

The **Rules** tab has **Applies to** and a notice that the candidate is emailed when the step becomes their turn. An AI interview is always visible to the candidate.

## Try as candidate

Click **Try as candidate** in the **Preview as a candidate** box to take the interview yourself with your current settings, including unsaved changes. Nothing is kept when you close it.

<Note>
**Try as candidate** uses credits, the same as a real interview. So does the play button that previews a voice.
</Note>

## What the candidate sees before starting

Before they start, the candidate must check a box confirming they understand the session is recorded, assessed by AI and affects their match score. A **Details** popover explains more:

- Chat saves what they type. Voice also records audio. Video records audio and picture.
- An AI reads the transcript and writes an assessment for your team, with a suggested next step. The candidate doesn't see it.
- The recording, transcript and assessment are deleted six months after the application closes.

For Voice and Video, they also check their microphone and camera, and can choose to hold a key to talk instead of leaving the mic on.

If their connection drops, their answers are already saved. A **Send again** button appears for the message that failed. Reloading the page takes them back into the same interview.

Once it's complete, the candidate can't retake it on their own. To let them try again, use **Reset step** in the [Step actions menu](/companies/run-the-process). It deletes the previous attempt.

## What you get afterward

When the candidate finishes, their step on the **Process** tab shows:

- The transcript.
- An evaluation: a short summary, strengths, concerns and a suggested next step of **Advance**, **Uncertain** or **Reject**.
- A recording, for Voice and Video.
- The candidate's rating of the experience, if they left one.

Staffer then rescores the candidate. The rescore reads what the candidate said. The AI's evaluation doesn't count toward the score. See [How match scores work](/companies/match-scores).

If a candidate leaves without answering anything, the step still completes, with no transcript and no evaluation.

## Retention and cost

The recording, transcript and evaluation are deleted six months after the application closes, not six months after the interview. The record that the candidate confirmed the notice is kept.

AI interviews use your organization's credits, and so do voice previews and **Try as candidate**. See [Credits, top-ups and spend caps](/admin/credits).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the candidate see their own evaluation?">
    No. Only your team sees the summary and suggested next step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a candidate retake an AI interview?">
    Not on their own. Use **Reset step** to let them try again. It deletes the previous attempt.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Try as candidate cost anything?">
    Yes. It uses credits, the same as a real interview, even though nothing is saved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the AI's own verdict affect the match score?">
    No. Only what the candidate said counts as evidence. The AI's summary and suggested next step are left out of scoring on purpose.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
