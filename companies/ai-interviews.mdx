An AI interview step runs in one of three modes: chat, voice, or video. Pick the mode when you build the step.

## The three modes

Chat is a written back-and-forth. Voice is a live spoken conversation. Video adds a recorded feed on top of the conversation. All three run the same underlying interview logic, just through a different medium.

## Voice and pacing

Voice and video interviews use voice models that vary tone and inflection through the conversation. The agent leaves room between responses so the candidate has time to think, and it ignores thinking sounds and filler words instead of treating them as an answer.

## Step settings

Open the job's **Process** tab and click an AI interview step to open its settings. The **Setup** tab has:

- **Mode.** Chat, voice, or video.
- **Language.** The language the interview runs in.
- **Voice.** Pick the interviewer's voice, and press play next to it to hear a sample. New steps start with updated default voices and tone. Lauren, a US voice, is now available.
- **Speaking speed.** A slider from slower to faster. The default is 0.85x, a little below normal.
- **Max length (minutes).** A hard cap. When it's reached, the AI wraps up the interview and hands off to the rating screen.
- **Interviewer tone.** Sets how the AI runs the conversation.

Click **Try as candidate** to run the interview yourself. You don't need to save the step first.

Voice and speaking speed only affect voice and video interviews.

The preview uses the same header as the apply page, so you see what the candidate sees.

## What the candidate is told

Before starting, the candidate sees a disclosure that the session is recorded, a transcript is kept, and an AI evaluation is produced for the recruiter, which the candidate does not see themselves. They have to acknowledge it before the interview can start.

## What you get afterward

A transcript and an AI evaluation land on the candidate's process step once the interview finishes, and their match score updates to take it into account. Audio tags are stripped from the transcript, so it reads as what was said.

<Info>
  Interview recordings, transcripts, and evaluations are deleted automatically six months after the application closes.
</Info>
