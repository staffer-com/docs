**Settings** → **Workspace** → **Members** lists everyone with access to your workspace, their role, and any invitations still waiting to be accepted. Everyone in the workspace can see this list. Only admins and owners can invite someone, change a role, or remove someone.

## The members list

Search by name or email, or use **Filter** to narrow by role. Each row shows the person's name (yours is tagged **You**), their role, and when they joined. Admins and owners get a **⋯** menu on each row to change a role or remove someone. It doesn't appear on your own row or the workspace owner's row.

## Invite a teammate

Click **Invite teammate**. See [Invite your team](/companies/invite-team) for the invite form, the roles it offers, and what the invited person sees.

## Changing a role

From a row's **⋯** menu, pick **Admin**, **Member**, **Reviewer**, or **Viewer**. You can't change the workspace owner's role from here, or your own. If someone holds the **owner** role in your organization, their role can't be changed from this screen either, no matter what role they hold in this particular workspace.

## Removing someone

**Remove from workspace** takes away their access immediately. The same owner and organization-owner protections apply: neither can be removed from this screen.

## Pending invitations

Each pending invite shows the email, when it was sent, and whether it's expired. **Resend** sends a new email and gives the invite another 14 days from now. The **×** button cancels the invite.

<Note>
There's no self-service way to transfer workspace ownership. The owner's row never shows a way to change or remove it from this screen.
</Note>

## What each role can do

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything an Admin can, plus delete the workspace and change its public URL slug. |
| Admin | Everything a Member can, plus edit workspace settings, manage members and invitations, delete any job and move jobs to another workspace. |
| Member | Create and edit jobs, search for, shortlist and promote candidates, message candidates, publish listings and close jobs. |
| Reviewer, Viewer | Read-only, except they can complete a process step they're named on and revoke share links. |

Reviewer and Viewer have the same access today. Naming someone on a step is what lets them complete it, not the Reviewer role.

See [Roles and permissions](/get-started/roles-and-permissions) for the full breakdown of what each role can do.

## Organization roles are separate

Owner, admin, and member at the organization level control billing, compliance, and settings that span workspaces, not what someone can do inside this one. See [Organization settings](/admin/organization).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I invite someone directly as an owner?">
    No. The invite form offers Admin, Member, Reviewer or Viewer. The owner is the person who created the workspace.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm an admin. Why can't I remove this person?">
    You can't remove the workspace owner, or anyone who's an owner of your organization, regardless of their role in this workspace.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a Reviewer and a Viewer?">
    Nothing, in terms of access. Both are read-only unless personally named on a process step.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
