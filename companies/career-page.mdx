Every workspace has a public career page listing its open, published roles.

## Branding

Set your logo, cover image, and primary color from Settings. These apply across your career page and your published listings together, not just one or the other.

## Offices

Add your office locations so candidates can see where you're based before they apply.

## What shows up

Only published listings appear. Draft and closed listings stay off the page entirely, whether or not they're anonymous.
