Compliance settings are visible to organization admins. If you can't see this section, your organization's admin can grant access.

## What's there

A Data Processing Addendum summary, a full list of sub-processors tagged by where candidate data is processed, and your organization's policies and legal documents, all viewable in-app rather than scattered across emailed PDFs.

## Sub-processor changes

Adding or replacing a sub-processor isn't silent. You get an in-app notice at least 30 days before it takes effect.
