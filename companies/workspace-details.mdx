Step 2 of 3. After your workspace is created, confirm the details Staffer will use across the app: in job listings, in the AI copilot's context, and on your career page.

## The details form

Review and edit:

- Company name and website
- Industry, size, and headquarters location
- A short description
- Branding: logo, cover image, and primary color

Everything here can be changed later from Settings.

## Automatic research

When you submit the form, Staffer starts a research pass over public information about your company and begins pulling out facts, things like funding stage, product area, or notable customers, to save you typing them in by hand.

You'll see facts appear one at a time as they're found, usually finishing in under a minute. If it's taking longer, you can continue without waiting: research keeps running in the background, and nothing about it blocks the rest of setup.

<Note>
  Research isn't the last word. Everything it finds can be reviewed, corrected, or removed afterward from your workspace settings, and it only ever adds to your profile. It never overwrites what you've already entered by hand.
</Note>
