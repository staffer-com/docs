Candidates reach your jobs through your career page, the public job board at jobs.staffer.com, or a listing link you share directly. Every listing has one apply button, and it works the same everywhere: on the job board, on your career page and in the candidate's own Staffer dashboard.

## Two ways to apply

After clicking apply, the candidate picks how to send their application.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="With their Staffer résumé">
    For a candidate who signs in with Google, Microsoft or LinkedIn. A candidate who hasn't set up their Staffer profile does that first. Staffer then sends their profile, so there's no upload or form.

    A second application this way to the same job is refused: "You already applied to this role."
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By uploading a CV">
    The candidate uploads a CV (PDF or `.docx`, up to 10 MB) and checks their **Name** and **Email**. Staffer reads the CV to fill them in, but never overwrites what the candidate typed. An uploaded CV is scored and evaluated like any other application.

    A second submission with the same email to the same job is caught: it offers **Resend my link** instead of creating a new application. A different email is treated as a separate candidate, even with an identical CV.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

There's no free-text message box on the application. Every application already gets an acknowledgement.

## The privacy notice

Candidates aren't asked to tick a consent box to apply. A privacy notice tells them how their details are used, and it never blocks an application. If you've turned on your own candidate privacy policy (**Settings** → **Organization** → **Compliance** → **Policies**), it's linked right next to Staffer's own. If you haven't turned it on, only Staffer's policy is linked. See [AI policies](/admin/ai-policies).

The only consent candidates are asked for is optional: whether you can consider them for future roles at your company.

An [anonymous listing](/companies/anonymous-listings) never links your privacy policy, because it would name your company.

## What the candidate gets afterward

A candidate who uploads a CV goes straight to their private application page, and gets an email confirming the application with the private link. They use the link, without an account, to check their status, message you and complete process steps. On an anonymous listing, the email doesn't name your company.

A candidate who applies while signed in doesn't get an email. The application appears in their Staffer account. See [Applying to a job](/candidates/applying-to-a-job) and [Tracking your application](/candidates/tracking-your-application).

## How it lands in your workspace

The candidate appears on the job's **Candidates** tab and on the **Applications** page, at **In review**, the same as a candidate you promote.

- Your job's [process steps](/companies/build-a-process) start right away, the same as for any other candidate.
- Staffer scores them within moments. You don't need to click **Rescore**.
- Your **Candidates** and **Applications** lists show it under **Source** as **Applied**, distinct from **Sourced** (you found them) or **Manual** (you added them by hand).
- You get a "New application" notification if you're subscribed to the job and have **New application in your workspace** turned on. You're subscribed to jobs you create. For other jobs, turn on **Notify me** in the job's **…** menu.

<Note>
  If Staffer can't read the résumé, it still creates the application from what the candidate typed and keeps the file. The profile has less detail until you click **Re-process résumé** on the file in the candidate's profile panel.
</Note>

## Closed jobs

Once a job is closed, its listing can stay visible for a while, but candidates can't apply. The page shows **Applications closed** instead of the apply button. See [Close, reopen or delete a job](/companies/close-reopen-delete).

## What this doesn't do

Staffer doesn't post your listing to LinkedIn, Indeed or any other job board. The **External jobs** on the public board at jobs.staffer.com are postings Staffer brings in from elsewhere, not your listings going out.

There's no CAPTCHA. The résumé form checks for bots in the background and limits how often someone can submit.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can the same person apply twice with different emails?">
    Yes, and Staffer won't catch it. Staffer doesn't compare applications across the two ways of applying or across different emails, so you can end up with two applications from the same person.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does applying with a Staffer résumé require an account?">
    Yes. The candidate signs in with Google, Microsoft or LinkedIn, which creates a Staffer account if they don't have one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a candidate who applies directly ever sit in Shortlist first?">
    No. The shortlist is only for people you find in a search. Anyone who applies starts at **In review**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
