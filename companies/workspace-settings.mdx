Unlike your account settings, these apply to everyone in the workspace.

## Job and sourcing defaults

Set defaults like how many results a search pulls back, so every new job starts from the same baseline instead of everyone configuring it by hand.

## Message templates

Save reusable outreach templates so recruiters aren't writing the same message from scratch every time.

## Branding and career page

Your logo, cover image, and primary color. See [Your career page](/companies/career-page) for where these show up.

## Knowledge

The facts Staffer knows about your company, from research plus anything you've added by hand. See [Add your company details](/companies/workspace-details).
