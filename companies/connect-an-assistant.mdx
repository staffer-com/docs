You can connect your own AI assistant to a Staffer workspace and review jobs, candidates and your process without leaving it. This is in beta.

This is separate from [the agent](/companies/the-agent), which lives inside Staffer. A connected assistant is the Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor you already use, reading from and working in Staffer for you.

## Which assistants work

- Claude
- ChatGPT
- Cursor
- VS Code with GitHub Copilot
- Gemini CLI

**Settings** → **Connected apps** has a **Connect an assistant** guide for each one. Staffer connects through MCP (Model Context Protocol), the standard these assistants use for connectors.

Staffer isn't listed in the Claude or ChatGPT directories yet, so you add it by hand with the guide.

## Connect an assistant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the guide">
    Go to **Settings** → **Connected apps** and follow the **Connect an assistant** guide for your assistant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a workspace and an access level">
    When you connect, you pick one workspace and choose read-only or read-and-write access. The assistant only works in the workspace you picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask it something">
    Ask about your jobs and candidates the way you'd ask a colleague. Every answer links straight back to the right page in Staffer, so you can check it there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What it can do

What the assistant can do follows your role in Staffer, and Staffer checks it on every request. A viewer who connects with read-and-write access still can't change anything a viewer can't change in the app. See [Roles and permissions](/get-started/roles-and-permissions).

Staffer also gives the assistant ready-made prompts for common jobs:

- A weekly review of your jobs and candidates
- Reviewing a job
- Preparing for an interview
- Shortlisting
- Drafting outreach

Drafted outreach stays a draft. The assistant never sends it.

## What still needs your approval

The assistant can't do these on its own:

- Move a candidate to a different stage
- Send a message
- Close a job
- Send a share link
- Publish a listing

For each one, it asks you to approve it on a Staffer page first. One approval covers exactly one action. The assistant suggests the action, and you decide whether it happens.

## Disconnect an assistant

Go to **Settings** → **Connected apps** and click **Disconnect** next to the assistant. You can disconnect at any time.

## The Staffer API

The actions the agent and connected assistants use are also available as a public API, so your own integrations and scripts can build on Staffer directly.

- It covers jobs, process steps, listings, searches, share links, applications, candidate profiles, the talent pool, segments, workspace settings, your own profile and calendar, analytics, billing and compliance.
- Sign-in uses OAuth 2.1. What an integration can do follows the signed-in person's role, the same as in the app.
- A full OpenAPI 3.1 description lets you generate client code automatically.
- Retries are safe. Repeating a create or a send with the same key never creates a second record or sends a second email.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can the assistant do more than my role allows?">
    No. Staffer checks your role on every request, whatever access level you picked when you connected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will the assistant message candidates on its own?">
    No. Sending a message needs your approval on a Staffer page, one message at a time. Outreach it drafts is never sent from the assistant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it see every workspace I'm in?">
    No. It only works in the one workspace you picked when you connected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I find Staffer in Claude's or ChatGPT's directory?">
    It isn't listed there yet. Follow the **Connect an assistant** guide in **Settings** → **Connected apps** to add it by hand.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
