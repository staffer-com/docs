You can connect your own AI assistant to a Staffer workspace and review jobs, candidates and your process without leaving it. This is in beta.

This is separate from [the agent](/companies/the-agent), which lives inside Staffer. A connected assistant is the Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor you already use, reading from and working in Staffer for you.

## Which assistants work

- Claude
- ChatGPT
- Claude Code
- Cursor
- VS Code with GitHub Copilot
- Gemini CLI

Click **MCP** at the bottom of the sidebar, or go to **Settings** → **Account** → **Connected apps**, then click **Connect an assistant**. It shows the server URL to paste, `https://app.staffer.com/api/mcp`, and the steps for each assistant. Staffer connects through MCP (Model Context Protocol), the standard these assistants use for connectors.

Staffer isn't listed in the Claude or ChatGPT directories yet, so you add it as a custom connector. On a Claude Team or Enterprise plan, an owner may need to add the connector first. In ChatGPT, you may need to turn on developer mode, depending on your plan.

## Turn it on for your organization

Connected assistants are off until an organization owner or admin turns them on. Go to **Settings** → **Organization** → **AI assistants** and switch on **Allow connected AI assistants**. While it's off, **Connect an assistant** is hidden, and **Connected apps** reads "Connected AI assistants are turned off for your organisation."

<Note>
  The switch started off for every organization on 24 October 2026. Assistants connected before then stopped working until an admin turns it on.
</Note>

## Connect an assistant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the guide">
    Click **Connect an assistant**, pick your assistant and follow its steps. Each one ends on a Staffer page that opens in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a workspace and an access level">
    On that page, choose the **Workspace** the assistant can use. It can always read jobs, candidates and searches. Turn on **Also make changes** to let it shortlist and move candidates and edit jobs. Then click **Connect**. The assistant only works in the workspace you picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask it something">
    Ask about your jobs and candidates the way you'd ask a colleague. Every answer links straight back to the right page in Staffer, so you can check it there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What it can do

What the assistant can do follows your role in Staffer, and Staffer checks it on every request. A viewer who connects with read-and-write access still can't change anything a viewer can't change in the app, and an assistant only sees the jobs you can see. See [Roles and permissions](/get-started/roles-and-permissions).

Staffer also gives the assistant ready-made prompts for common jobs:

- A weekly review of your jobs and candidates
- Reviewing a job
- Preparing for an interview
- Shortlisting
- Drafting outreach

Drafted outreach stays a draft. The assistant never sends it.

Every call an assistant makes is recorded in your organization's audit log: which assistant, what it did and how many records came back, but not the content.

## What still needs your approval

The assistant can't do these on its own, even with **Also make changes** on:

- Move a candidate to a different stage
- Send a message
- Close a job
- Create or send a share link
- Publish a listing
- Delete anything, such as a note, a step or a job

For each one, it sends you to a Staffer page titled with the assistant's name and "wants to do this". The page says exactly what will happen, in which workspace. Click **Approve** or **Decline**. An approval covers that one action, once, and runs out after 10 minutes. The assistant suggests the action, and you decide whether it happens.

## Disconnect an assistant

Go to **Settings** → **Account** → **Connected apps**. Each assistant shows its workspace, whether it can make changes, and when it was connected and last used. Click **Disconnect** next to it. The assistant loses access from its next request.

A connection also ends on its own:

- After 90 days at most, however often you use it. Connect again to keep going.
- When you reset your Staffer password. That disconnects all your assistants.
- When an organization admin disconnects it, or turns **Allow connected AI assistants** off.

## The Staffer API

The actions the agent and connected assistants use are also available as a public API, so your own integrations and scripts can build on Staffer directly.

- It covers jobs, process steps, listings, searches, share links, applications, candidate profiles, the talent pool, segments, workspace settings, your own profile and calendar, analytics, billing and compliance.
- Sign-in uses OAuth 2.1. What an integration can do follows the signed-in person's role, the same as in the app.
- A full OpenAPI 3.1 description lets you generate client code automatically.
- Retries are safe. Repeating a create or a send with the same key never creates a second record or sends a second email.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I find Connect an assistant?">
    Your organization hasn't turned connected assistants on. An organization owner or admin can switch on **Allow connected AI assistants** in **Settings** → **Organization** → **AI assistants**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the assistant do more than my role allows?">
    No. Staffer checks your role on every request, whatever access level you picked when you connected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will the assistant message candidates on its own?">
    No. Sending a message needs your approval on a Staffer page, one message at a time. Outreach it drafts is never sent from the assistant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it see every workspace I'm in?">
    No. It only works in the one workspace you picked when you connected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I find Staffer in Claude's or ChatGPT's directory?">
    It isn't listed there yet. Add it as a custom connector, following the **Connect an assistant** steps in **Settings** → **Account** → **Connected apps**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
