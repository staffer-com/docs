Staffer has three plans: **Starter**, **Growth**, and **Enterprise**. All three draw from the same shared pool of credits. What changes between them is monthly commitment, bonus credits, and how many workspaces you can run.

## The three plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | Free | $100 in signup credits, 1 workspace, no monthly commitment, top up any time |
| **Growth** | $3,000 / month | 5% bonus credits on every top-up, $3,150 credited monthly, unlimited workspaces, cancel any time with coverage to period end |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Negotiated bonus (typically 10%+), bank-transfer invoicing, unlimited workspaces, priority support and activation by the Staffer team |

## How the bonus works

The bonus applies to every dollar you pay Staffer, not only your monthly commitment. A one-off top-up and your monthly subscription invoice both get your plan's bonus percentage.

On **Growth**, that bonus is 5%. Top up $1,000 and you get $1,050 in credits: $1,000 base plus $50 bonus. The same 5% is why the $3,000 monthly commitment lands as $3,150, the number shown on the Growth tile itself.

**Starter** carries no bonus. **Enterprise**'s bonus is set in your contract, typically 10% or more.

## Upgrading to Growth

From **Settings** → **Organization** → **Billing**, click **Manage plan**. That opens the Plans page. Choosing **Growth** takes you to Stripe's hosted checkout to add a card and start the subscription. Once it's active, every future top-up gets the 5% bonus, and your first invoice credits $3,150 to the shared pool.

## Downgrading or cancelling

Click **Downgrade to Starter**. This doesn't cut you off immediately: coverage runs to the end of your current billing period, then the plan lapses to **Starter**. Changed your mind before the period ends? Click **Resume plan** to stay on **Growth** without interruption.

Downgrading doesn't touch workspaces you already have. On **Starter** you can't create another workspace beyond your first, but any extra workspaces you made while on **Growth** keep working. Nothing gets deleted or locked.

## Starter's one-workspace limit

**Starter** caps an organization at 1 workspace. Trying to add another from the workspace switcher (**+ New workspace**) is disabled, with a prompt to upgrade. See [Workspaces](/admin/workspaces) for how workspace creation works day to day.

## Enterprise

**Enterprise** isn't self-serve. Click **Contact sales** and Staffer sets up your plan, your bonus rate, and bank-transfer invoicing, and activates it for you.
