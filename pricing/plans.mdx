Staffer has four plans: **Starter**, **Team**, **Pro** and **Enterprise**. They differ in how many active jobs they include and what you pay per job. Prices are in USD, per month.

## The four plans

| Plan | Active jobs | Price per job | Monthly | Annual | Searches a month | AI interviews a month |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | 1 | \$399 | \$399 | \$4,788 | 50 | 100 |
| **Team** | 3 | \$349 | \$1,047 | \$12,564 | 150 | 300 |
| **Pro** | 10 | \$299 | \$2,990 | \$35,880 | 500 | 1,000 |
| **Enterprise** | More than 10 | Custom | Custom | Custom | 50 per job | 100 per job |

Annual is 12 times the monthly price, with no discount. Every plan includes as many members as you invite. A job counts as active while it's **Qualifying** or **Open**. See [How pricing works](/pricing/how-it-works).

## Pick a plan

Match the plan to how many jobs you run at the same time.

| Active jobs | Plan | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Starter** | \$399 |
| 2 | **Starter** plus 1 extra job | \$798 |
| 3 to 8 | **Team**, plus an extra job from the 4th | \$1,047 to \$2,792 |
| 9 or 10 | **Pro** | \$2,990 |
| More than 10 | **Pro** plus extra jobs, or **Enterprise** | From \$3,289 on **Pro** |

Two jobs on **Starter** cost less than **Team**. From 9 jobs, **Pro** costs less than **Team** plus extras.

## Add-ons

| Add-on | Price | Per unit |
|---|---|---|
| 50 extra searches | \$100 | \$2.00 per search |
| 100 extra AI interviews | \$150 | \$1.50 per interview |
| Extra active job | Your plan's price per job, per month | \$399 on **Starter**, \$349 on **Team**, \$299 on **Pro** |

An extra active job also adds 50 searches and 100 AI interviews a month to your pool.

## Examples

| You run | Plan | Monthly | Annual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 active jobs | **Team** plus 1 extra job: 4 × \$349 | \$1,396 | \$16,752 |
| 5 active jobs | **Team** plus 2 extra jobs: 5 × \$349 | \$1,745 | \$20,940 |
| 9 active jobs | **Pro** | \$2,990 | \$35,880 |

With 4 active jobs on **Team**, your organization gets 200 searches and 400 AI interviews a month.

## Change your plan

From **Settings** → **Organization** → **Billing**, click **Manage plan**. That opens the Plans page. Pick a plan and Staffer takes you to Stripe's hosted checkout to add a card and start the subscription. You add extra jobs and buy add-ons from **Billing** too.

Cancelling doesn't cut you off. Your plan runs to the end of the current billing period, and you can click **Resume plan** before then to keep it.

Only organization owners and admins can open **Billing** or change the plan.

## Enterprise

**Enterprise** is for more than 10 active jobs. The price per job is set in your contract, and each job adds 50 searches and 100 AI interviews a month to your pool. Staffer invoices your organization directly and you pay by bank transfer.

**Enterprise** isn't self-serve. Click **Contact sales** and Staffer sets up your plan and activates it for you. Staffer can also build a custom plan with its own number of jobs, searches and AI interviews.

  <Accordion title="Is paying annually cheaper?">
    No. Annual is 12 times the monthly price.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if we close a job?">
    It stops counting as active. You can open another job in its place without paying for an extra one.
  </Accordion>
