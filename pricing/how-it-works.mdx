---
title: "How pricing works"
description: "A monthly plan priced per active job, with one shared pool of searches and AI interviews."
icon: "credit-card"
---

You pay per active job, per month. Each job on your plan adds 50 searches and 100 AI interviews to one pool your whole organization shares. Everything else in Staffer comes with the plan.

## Active jobs

A job is active while it's **Qualifying** or **Open**. Only active jobs count toward your plan, and your plan counts how many are active at the same time.

**Draft**, **Planned** and **Closed** jobs cost nothing. Close a job when you're done hiring and it stops counting, so you can open another in its place.

<Note>
A new job starts as **Open**, so it counts from the moment the agent drafts its brief. A duplicated job is **Open** too. See [Create a job](/companies/create-a-job).
</Note>

Your plan includes 1 active job on **Starter**, 3 on **Team** and 10 on **Pro**. Each extra active job costs your plan's price per job, per month. See [Plans](/pricing/plans).

## Searches and AI interviews

Each job your plan includes adds 50 searches and 100 AI interviews a month to one pool. The pool belongs to your organization, so every workspace and every job draws from it. A busy job can use more than its share while a quiet one uses less.

| Plan | Active jobs | Searches a month | AI interviews a month |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | 1 | 50 | 100 |
| **Team** | 3 | 150 | 300 |
| **Pro** | 10 | 500 | 1,000 |
| **Enterprise** | More than 10 | 50 per job | 100 per job |

Extra jobs add to the pool too. **Team** with 1 extra job gets 200 searches and 400 AI interviews a month.

Need more? Buy 50 extra searches for \$100, or 100 extra AI interviews for \$150. See [Add-ons](/pricing/plans#add-ons).

## Unused allowance carries over one month

Anything you don't use in a month carries over to the next one: active jobs your plan includes but you didn't fill, searches and AI interviews. If you don't use it that month either, it expires.

## What's included

Everything apart from searches and AI interviews comes with your plan, with no meter. That covers chatting with the agent, scoring and rescoring candidates, generating and translating listings, drafting messages, interview notetaking and company research.

Candidate chat is free for your organization. A candidate chatting on the apply page, in the candidate portal or through a share link doesn't use your plan.

Everyday work doesn't touch the pool either: viewing jobs and candidates, editing a brief, building a process, adding a note, sending a message you wrote yourself.

## No seats

You don't pay per member. Invite as many people as you need: adding a teammate doesn't change your bill.

## Free trial

New organizations start on a free trial, so you can try Staffer before you pick a plan.

## Annual billing

Paying yearly costs 12 times the monthly price. There's no discount for paying annually.

Only organization owners and admins can choose or change the plan. See [Plans](/pricing/plans).
