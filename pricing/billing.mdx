**Settings** → **Organization** → **Billing** holds your organization's plan, payment method, billing details, and invoices. Only organization owners and admins can open it.

## Current plan

Shows your plan's name, price, and highlights, plus a badge: **Current plan**, or **Ending** with a date if you've cancelled and coverage is running out its period. Click **Manage plan** to upgrade, downgrade, or contact sales for Enterprise. See [Plans](/pricing/plans).

## Payment method

Until you add one, this reads "No card on file." Click **Add payment method** and Staffer takes you to Stripe's hosted checkout to save a card. You need a card on file before you can add credits. See [Credits, top-ups and spend caps](/admin/credits).

Once a card is saved, this section shows its brand, last 4 digits, and expiry, with **Replace card** and **Remove card** actions. Removing a card doesn't touch your existing credits: it only detaches the payment method from Stripe.

## Bank transfer

On **Enterprise**, Staffer invoices your organization directly and collects by bank transfer instead of a card, with no card fees.

## Billing details

Legal company name, country, address, and a tax ID (Staffer supports EU VAT, UK VAT, US EIN, and over a dozen other country-specific tax ID types). Stripe Tax uses your country and tax ID to compute VAT automatically on every invoice. This section stays disabled until you've added a card, since these details attach to the same Stripe customer record as your card.

## Billing manager email

One address on file. It's shown to your team when a workspace runs out of credits, as the place to ask for a top-up, and it separately receives billing notifications like low-balance alerts. Only organization owners and admins can change it.

## Invoices

**Recent invoices**, on the Billing page itself, shows your last 3. **All invoices** lists up to 50, each with its date, number, amount, status, a PDF, and either **View** (once paid) or **Pay** (while open) linking to Stripe's hosted invoice page.

## Currency

Your credits, spend caps, and top-ups are always priced in USD. An individual Stripe invoice can show a different currency if that's how it was billed, but the credit pool itself is USD only.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is Billing details greyed out?">
    It needs a card on file first, because it saves onto the same Stripe customer record as your payment method. Add a card, and the fields open up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does removing my card affect my credits?">
    No. Removing a card only detaches it from Stripe. Your credits and past invoices are untouched, and you can add a new card any time.
  </Accordion>
