Payments run through **Stripe**, by card. Free top-ups and Growth are charged to the card on file.

## Enterprise invoicing

Enterprise agreements can be invoiced on standard payment terms instead of card.

## Currency

Pricing is set in USD. If your card is issued in another currency, Stripe converts at payment, so you can pay from a EUR or NOK account with no extra setup.
