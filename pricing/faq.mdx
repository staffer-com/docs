description: "Real questions about active jobs, searches, AI interviews, carry-over and invoices."
icon: "circle-help"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which jobs do we pay for?">
    Only active jobs: ones that are **Qualifying** or **Open**. **Draft**, **Planned** and **Closed** jobs cost nothing. A new job starts as **Open**, so it counts right away. See [How pricing works](/pricing/how-it-works).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do unused searches and AI interviews expire?">
    They carry over one month, then expire. The same goes for active jobs your plan includes but you didn't fill.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are searches and AI interviews per job or shared?">
    Shared. Each job on your plan adds 50 searches and 100 AI interviews a month to one pool for your whole organization. Any job in any workspace can draw from it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if we need more searches or AI interviews?">
    Buy an add-on: 50 extra searches for \$100, or 100 extra AI interviews for \$150. An extra active job also adds 50 searches and 100 AI interviews. See [Add-ons](/pricing/plans#add-ons).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does chatting with the agent or scoring cost extra?">
    No. Agent chat, scoring, rescoring, listing drafts, message drafts, interview notetaking and company research are included in every plan. Only searches and AI interviews draw on the pool.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a per-seat fee?">
    No. Staffer doesn't charge per member on any plan. Inviting more teammates doesn't change your bill.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is paying annually cheaper?">
    No. Annual is 12 times the monthly price.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can we pay by invoice?">
    On **Enterprise**, yes, by bank transfer. **Starter**, **Team** and **Pro** pay by card through Stripe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who gets billing emails?">
    Every organization owner and admin, plus your **Billing manager email** if one is set. See [Payment and invoices](/pricing/billing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can we get a refund?">
    Only Staffer can issue one. There's no self-serve refund button in the app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do candidates cost us anything?">
    Only through AI interviews. Each one a candidate takes uses one of your organization's AI interviews. Candidate chat on the apply page, in the candidate portal and on share links is free for your organization, and so are applying and completing other steps.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
