|  | Per hire |
| --- | --- |
| Fully loaded in-house hire | \$4,700 |
| Agency on a hard role | \$5-30,000\+ |
| **Staffer** | ~**\$1,285** |

Three to four times cheaper than a fully loaded in-house hire. Up to twenty-five times cheaper than an agency placement on a hard role.

## What drives that number

| Work | Unit | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI sourcing | search round | ~\$12 |
| Match scoring | applicant scored | ~\$1 |
| AI interview | interview completed | ~\$8 |
| Enrichment | email \+ phone reveal | ~\$1.50 |
| Next-action prep | action prepared | ~\$0.40 |
| Agent actions | action run | ~\$0.20 |
